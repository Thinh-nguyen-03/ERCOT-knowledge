## Definition

The State of the Market report is the annual report the Independent Market Monitor (IMM) submits to the PUCT assessing how the ERCOT wholesale market performed and recommending changes. PUCT rule 25.365 requires an annual report on the state of the market covering competitiveness, ERCOT's management of the balancing energy, ancillary services and congestion rights markets, congestion management, flaws and opportunities for manipulation in the market design, and recommendations for improving it. [e1]

## How it works

The IMM is a market monitor that ERCOT contracts with, selected by the commission, to detect and prevent market manipulation strategies, recommend ways to improve efficiency and give independent analysis of proposed market changes. [e3] Under rule 25.365 all of its reports must reflect its independent analysis, findings and expertise, and it prepares and submits the annual report to the commission alongside other price and congestion reports. [e2][e1]

The 2025 report, issued in May 2026, is written by Potomac Economics as IMM and presents its assessment of the 2025 wholesale market outcomes plus recommended changes. [e4] The recommendations are numbered by the year first introduced and the order within that report; the 2025 report has one new recommendation, on the Firm Fuel Supply Service, plus outstanding ones from prior years and some being retired. [e5]

## ERCOT context

The report is the main public record of annual market outcomes and the IMM's design agenda. For 2025 it reports average electricity demand about 6.1% higher than in 2024, an increase of approximately 3,205 MW. [e6] It also highlights crypto-currency mining demand of about 4,600 MW in 2025, and says cryptocurrency mining accounted for more than half of ERS capacity by the end of 2025. [e7][e8]

## Key facts

- Legal basis: PUCT rule 25.365(k)(2) requires the IMM's annual state-of-the-market report. [e1]
- The report must assess market competitiveness, ERCOT's management of balancing energy, ancillary services and congestion rights markets, and design flaws. [e1]
- 2025 report: average demand up about 6.1% (about 3,205 MW) from 2024. [e6]
- 2025 report: one new recommendation (Firm Fuel Supply Service); recommendations are numbered by year introduced. [e5]
- IMM reports must reflect its independent analysis. [e2]

## Say it like this

(Guidance) The State of the Market report is the IMM's annual assessment of ERCOT's markets, delivered to the PUCT. Analysts use it for what actually happened in a year and for the IMM's list of design fixes.

## Common confusion

(Guidance) The IMM recommends but does not decide. The PUCT keeps enforcement authority and approves market design changes, so a recommendation in the report does not mean a rule change is coming. [e3] Also, this is the IMM's report, not ERCOT's. [e4]

## Questions

### Who requires the State of the Market report and who writes it?

PUCT rule 25.365 requires the IMM to submit it to the commission; Potomac Economics acts as the IMM. [e1][e4]

### What must the annual report cover?

Market competitiveness, ERCOT's management of balancing energy, ancillary services and congestion rights markets, congestion management effectiveness, design flaws and manipulation opportunities, and recommendations. [e1]

### How are the report's recommendations numbered?

By the year first introduced, then the order in that report; for example the 2025 report's one new recommendation concerns the Firm Fuel Supply Service. [e5]
