## Definition

The Texas Legislature enacts the statutes that govern the ERCOT market, including the Public Utility Regulatory Act (PURA). [e10] In PURA it found that electric services and their prices should be determined by customer choices and the normal forces of competition, except for transmission and distribution services and stranded-cost recovery. [e1]

## How it works

Legislation sets the structure around ERCOT. PURA creates the ERCOT board selection committee, with one appointee each from the governor, the lieutenant governor and the speaker of the house. [e3] It requires that new or revised protocols not take effect until the PUCT approves a market impact statement. [e4] The PUCT is itself subject to the Texas Sunset Act, and HB 1500 sets September 1, 2029 as the date in the abolition provision, under which the commission is abolished on that date unless continued in existence. [e5]

The Legislature also asks for information back. Each year by December 1 the PUCT must file a report with the legislature that includes estimated annual costs to load-serving entities of backing up dispatchable and non-dispatchable generation. [e2] The market monitor may appear and speak before the legislature without PUCT restriction. [e9]

## ERCOT context

Recent sessions have written market and reliability design directly into PURA. SB 6 (89th Legislature, 2025) added the Large Load Demand Management Service, which directs the PUCT to require ERCOT to develop a reliability service that competitively procures demand reductions from large loads of at least 75 megawatts in anticipated emergencies. [e6][e7] An earlier 2023 law, SB 1929, led to large flexible load registration, covering virtual currency mining facilities of more than 75 megawatts with interruptible load. [e8][e11] HB 1500 (88th Legislature, 2023) added PURA §39.1514 on PUCT directives to ERCOT. [e10]

## Key facts

- PURA states a policy preference for customer choice and competition, except for transmission and distribution and stranded-cost recovery. [e1]
- ERCOT board selection committee: one appointee each from the governor, lieutenant governor and house speaker. [e3]
- HB 1500 (2023) sets September 1, 2029 as the date in the PUCT's Sunset abolition provision (the commission is abolished on that date unless continued in existence). [e5]
- SB 6 (2025, effective June 20, 2025) added PURA §39.170 on large load demand management. [e6][e7]

## Say it like this

(Guidance) The Legislature sets the rules of the game in PURA and amends them by statute, as with the large-load rules in SB 6. Protocol changes still need PUCT approval before they take effect. [e4]

## Common confusion

(Guidance) A statute does not change the Protocols by itself. PURA directs the PUCT and ERCOT, and protocol changes still need PUCT approval before taking effect. [e4]

## Questions

### How does the Legislature influence ERCOT's governance?

Through PURA: it creates the board selection committee, requires a formal protocol process with PUCT approval, and makes the PUCT subject to the Texas Sunset Act. [e3][e4][e5]

### What did SB 6 add for large loads?

PURA §39.170, which requires a reliability service to competitively procure demand reductions from large load customers of at least 75 megawatts in an anticipated emergency. [e6][e7]
