## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e2): A Settlement Statement is the statement ERCOT issues showing a breakdown of administrative, miscellaneous and market charges for the applicable market services. [e1] There are separate statements for the Day-Ahead Market and the Real-Time Market, and their settlement and billing timelines are separate. [e2]
- **How it works #1** (cites e14, e10): ERCOT posts a Settlement Calendar on its website showing, for each Operating Day, when each scheduled DAM and RTM statement will be issued. [e14] Statements are posted to the MIS Certified Area. [e10]
- **How it works #2** (cites e3, e4, e5, e6, e7): The DAM gets a DAM Statement on the second Business Day after the Operating Day. [e3] The RTM gets a series of statements for each Operating Day: an Initial Statement on the fifth day after the Operating Day (next Business Day if that is not one), a Final Statement on the 55th day, and a True-Up Statement 180 days after the Operating Day if the IDR Meter Data Threshold is met. [e4][e5][e6] If the threshold is not met the True-Up comes when the data is available, and one is issued no later than 365 days after the Operating Day. [e7]
- **How it works #3** (cites e8, e9): Resettlement statements correct earlier ones. A resettlement for non-price errors is triggered when the error exceeds 4% of the RTM Statement amount and $400 (2% and $200 for the DAM), and is found and noticed within a year of the Operating Day. [e8][e9]
- **How it works #4** (cites e10, e11, e12): ERCOT then issues Settlement Invoices on a net basis on the same Business Day the statements are posted. [e10] Amounts owed to ERCOT are due at 1700 on the second Bank Business Day after the invoice date. [e11] A negative amount on a statement or invoice is a payment from ERCOT to the recipient; a positive amount is owed to ERCOT. [e12]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e6, e7, e13): The RTM True-Up is tied to ERCOT receiving and validating usage data from a threshold share of ESI IDs, so its timing can move. [e6][e7] A recipient is deemed to have validated a DAM Statement unless it raises a dispute or exception within ten Business Days of issue. [e13]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e3): - DAM Statement: second Business Day after the Operating Day. [e3]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e4): - RTM Initial Statement: fifth day after the Operating Day, moved to the next Business Day if needed. [e4]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e5): - RTM Final Statement: 55th day after the Operating Day. [e5]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e6, e7): - RTM True-Up: 180 days after the Operating Day if the IDR Meter Data Threshold is met; no later than 365 days. [e6][e7]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e8, e9): - RTM resettlement threshold: 4% and $400; DAM: 2% and $200. [e8][e9]
- **Key facts #6** (cites e11): - Payment is due 1700 on the second Bank Business Day after the invoice date. [e11]
- **Key facts #7** (cites e13): - Dispute window to avoid being deemed to have validated a DAM statement: ten Business Days. [e13]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e5, e6, e12): "Final" does not mean last. The RTM Final Statement is issued on the 55th day, but a True-Up Statement follows at 180 days or later. [e5][e6] Also, a negative statement amount means ERCOT pays you, not the other way round. [e12]
- **Questions #2** (cites e3, e4, e5, e6): DAM: the second Business Day after. RTM: an Initial Statement on the fifth day, a Final on day 55, and a True-Up at 180 days. [e3][e4][e5][e6]
- **Questions #4** (cites e6, e7): The IDR Meter Data Threshold: usage data validated for at least 99% of ESI IDs with a BUSIDRRQ Load Profile Type and at least 90% from each qualifying Meter Reading Entity. If not met, ERCOT issues it when data is available, and in any case by 365 days. [e6][e7]
- **Questions #6** (cites e13) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 9.14: Raise a dispute under Section 9.14; a DAM statement is deemed validated if you raise no dispute or exception within ten Business Days. [e13] The detailed dispute rules are in Section 9.14.

- **Link to rtm** (cites e4, e5, e8, e6): settles Real-Time Market (RTM): The RTM has Initial, Final, Resettlement and True-Up Statements. [e4][e5][e8][e6]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) A trade day is settled in stages: the DAM statement lands two Business Days later, the real-time initial statement about five days later, a final at day 55 and a true-up around day 180. Each stage can move your numbers, so we treat the early statements as provisional.
