## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) A trade day is settled in stages: the DAM statement lands two Business Days later, the real-time initial statement about five days later, a final at day 55 and a true-up around day 180. Each stage can move your numbers, so we treat the early statements as provisional, and the dispute clocks run on Business Days.
