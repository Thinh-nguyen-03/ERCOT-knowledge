## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to puct** (cites e5, e12): related PUCT (Public Utility Commission of Texas): HB 1500 (2023) is the latest amendment to the Sunset provision, under which the PUCT is abolished on September 1, 2029 unless continued in existence. [e5][e12]
- **Link to ercot** (cites e3, e4, e21): related ERCOT: PURA creates the ERCOT board selection committee, requires PUCT approval of a market impact statement before new or revised protocols take effect, and, as amended by HB 1500, says an independent organization certified by the PUCT is directly accountable to it. [e3][e4][e21]
- **Link to sb6** (cites e6, e7, e51): related SB 6 (2025) Large Load Legislation: SB 6 of the 89th Legislature added large-load provisions to PURA, signed and effective June 20, 2025. [e6][e7][e51]
- **Link to large-flexible-load** (cites e8): related Large Flexible Load (LFL): Large flexible load registration is a statutory requirement in PURA. [e8]
- **Link to imm** (cites e9): related Independent Market Monitor (Potomac Economics): PURA says the PUCT may not restrict the market monitor from speaking to the Legislature. [e9]
- **Link to nodal-protocols** (cites e4): related ERCOT Nodal Protocols: Statute requires protocols to take effect only after PUCT approval. [e4]
- **Link to texas-energy-fund** (cites e33, e37) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2023: related Texas Energy Fund: SB 2627 (2023) added Texas energy fund provisions to PURA Chapter 34, and SB 2268 (2025) amended it. [e33][e37]
- **Link to drrs** (cites e17): related DRRS (Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service): HB 1500 added the requirement for a dispatchable reliability reserve ancillary service program. [e17]
- **Link to emergency-pricing-program** (cites e25) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2021: related Emergency Pricing Program: SB 3 (2021) added PURA Sec. 39.160 requiring the PUCT to establish an emergency pricing program. [e25]
- **Link to winter-storm-uri** (cites e29, e31) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2021: related Winter Storm Uri (Feb 2021): HB 4492 (2021) authorized financing of the Winter Storm Uri default and uplift balances. [e29][e31]
- **Link to tdsp** (cites e47, e48): related TDSP (Transmission & Distribution Service Provider): SB 6 orders the PUCT to evaluate the four coincident peak method used to charge transmission costs to distribution providers. [e47][e48]
- **Link to load-shed** (cites e27, e28) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 38.076: related Load Shed / Rotating Outages: PURA Sec. 38.076, added by SB 3, requires a system to allocate load shedding among cooperatives, municipally owned utilities and TDUs. [e27][e28]
