## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to block-products** (cites e1, e2): related Block Products (5x16, 2x16, 7x8, ATC): The ICE ERCOT North contracts come as a peak and an off-peak monthly block. [e1][e2]
- **Link to hb-north** (cites e3): related HB_NORTH (North Hub): The ICE ERCOT North peak contract settles on the ERCOT North 345KV Hub real-time price. [e3]
- **Link to trading-hub** (cites e3): related Trading Hubs: ICE's reference price is an ERCOT-published hub settlement price. [e3]
- **Link to rtm** (cites e3): determines Real-Time Market (RTM): The peak contract settles against the real-time price published by ERCOT for the North 345KV Hub. [e3]
- **Link to dart-spread** (cites e3): related DART Spread: ICE futures settle against the real-time hub price, not a day-ahead price. [e3]
- **Link to retail-hedging** (cites e20): related Retail Load Hedging: The IMM lists participating in futures markets among the ways LSEs manage price exposure. [e20]
- **Link to forward-curve** (cites e5): related Forward Curve: ICE lists these monthly futures out to up to 50 consecutive monthly contract periods. [e5]
- **Link to heat-rate** (cites e14): related Heat Rate: The ICE Panhandle heat rate contract settles on an agreed heat rate times the NYMEX last-day settlement price. [e14]
- **Link to implied-heat-rate** (cites e14): related Implied Market Heat Rate: The ICE heat rate contract settles on an agreed heat rate multiplied by the NYMEX last-day settlement price. [e14]
- **Link to crr** (cites e10, e11, e17): contrasts-with CRR (Congestion Revenue Right): The ICE peak hours match the CRR 5x16 block hours, but ICE futures are exchange contracts and CRRs are auctioned by ERCOT. [e10][e11][e17]
- **Link to dam** (cites e19): contrasts-with Day-Ahead Market (DAM): The IMM names the day-ahead and CRR markets as ERCOT's two forward markets; the ICE contracts are not among them. [e19]
- **Link to ppa-vppa** (cites e20): related PPA & Virtual PPA: The IMM lists bilateral PPAs and futures as separate hedging routes for LSEs. [e20]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ICE lists monthly cash-settled ERCOT North power futures, peak and off-peak, that settle against ERCOT's real-time hub prices. ICE also has an OTC heat rate contract that prices power as a multiple of the gas settlement price.
