## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ERCOT's large load queue is several times its record peak, and it is mostly data centers. History says only part of the requested MW actually shows up and runs. The near-term grid has handled it so far. The real questions are how much of the queue gets built, and whether generation and transmission can keep up.
