## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): Solar is an Intermittent Renewable Resource: in the Protocols, a Generation Resource that can only produce energy from variable, uncontrollable sources such as wind, solar or run-of-the-river hydroelectricity. [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e8, e4, e5): The IMM says solar production peaks in the middle of the day. [e8] Its capacity factor is high at daily peak load (over 48% in 2025) but near zero at the evening net peak (1.7% in 2025). [e4] Its overall capacity factor was 23.8% in 2025, below wind's 31.6%. [e5]
- **How it works #2** (cites e3, e2): Because solar output is concentrated at midday, midday is also when curtailment is most likely, since simultaneous production peaks. [e3] Roughly 6% of solar generation was curtailed in 2025, which the IMM calls relatively low, partly because solar projects can be sited closer to load and are less prone to congestion than wind. [e2]
