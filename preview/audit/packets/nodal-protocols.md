## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e3): The ERCOT Protocols are the document adopted by ERCOT, created with representatives of all segments of Market Participants, containing the scheduling, operating, planning, reliability and Settlement (including Customer registration) policies, rules, guidelines, procedures, standards and criteria of ERCOT. [e1] Market Participants, the IMM and ERCOT must abide by them. [e3]
- **How it works #1** (cites e3, e2): The Protocols implement ERCOT's functions as the PUCT-certified Independent Organization for the ERCOT Region and as Program Administrator of the REC Program. [e3] The version in effect when an action is performed or not performed governs that action. [e2]
- **How it works #2** (cites e4, e6, e5): "Other Binding Documents" are the policies, guidelines, procedures, forms and applications that the ERCOT Board, TAC, other authorized ERCOT subcommittees or ERCOT may develop for implementing and operating under the Protocols. [e4] Together with the Protocols they make up the scheduling, operating, planning, reliability and Settlement rules that bind Market Participants under PURA section 39.151. [e6] If an Agreement conflicts with the Protocols, the Agreement prevails to the extent of the conflict. [e5]
- **How it works #3** (cites e9, e11, e12, e10, e13): Changes go through a Nodal Protocol Revision Request. [e9] PRS reviews and recommends action on revision requests, and after a TAC report and impact analysis the ERCOT Board reviews them at its next regularly scheduled meeting. [e11][e12] All revision requests require PUCT approval before implementation. [e10] An Urgent track exists where an existing Protocol or condition is impairing or could imminently impair reliability or market operations, or creates a discrepancy between a settlement formula and the Protocols. [e13]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e7, e8) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 39.151: The statute behind this is PURA section 39.151: protocols adopted by an independent organization are subject to commission oversight and may not take effect before commission approval, and revised protocols may not take effect until the commission approves a market impact statement. [e7][e8]
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e14) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 4.5: The published Protocol text carries boxes for revisions to apply "upon system implementation". For example, Section 4.5.1 of the DAM text contains a box beginning "NPRR1188: Replace paragraph (a) above with the following upon system implementation". [e14] Anything in such a box is pending, not current.
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - The Protocols cover scheduling, operating, planning, reliability and Settlement (including Customer registration) rules. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e3): - Market Participants, the IMM and ERCOT shall abide by the Protocols. [e3]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e2): - The version in effect at the time of an action governs it. [e2]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e4): - Other Binding Documents are policies, guidelines, procedures, forms or applications that the ERCOT Board, TAC, other authorized subcommittees or ERCOT may develop for the Protocols. [e4]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e10): - All revision requests need PUCT approval before implementation. [e10]
- **Key facts #6** (cites e7): - Statute: protocols may not take effect before commission approval. [e7]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e7, e6, e14): The Protocols are not the same as the PUCT's substantive rules or PURA, though the Protocols are subject to PUCT approval and rest on PURA. [e7][e6] Also, a pending NPRR in a box is not yet the rule: read the text outside the box. [e14]
- **Questions #2** (cites e3): Market Participants, the IMM and ERCOT. [e3]
- **Questions #4** (cites e9, e11, e12, e10): Someone submits an NPRR; PRS reviews it and recommends action, a TAC Report and Impact Analysis go to the ERCOT Board, which reviews them at its next regularly scheduled meeting, and the PUCT must approve before implementation. [e9][e11][e12][e10]
- **Questions #6** (cites e7) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 39.151: PURA section 39.151 says protocols adopted by an independent organization are subject to commission oversight and may not take effect before commission approval. [e7]
