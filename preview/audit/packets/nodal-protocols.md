## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **How it works #3** (cites e4, e16, e17, e18): "Other Binding Documents" are the policies, guidelines, procedures, forms and applications that the ERCOT Board, TAC, other authorized ERCOT subcommittees or ERCOT may develop for implementing and operating under the Protocols. [e4] They exclude ERCOT's internal administrative procedures needed for its role as Independent Organization or as a NERC-registered entity. [e16] ERCOT posts the list and the documents, and a TAC designated subcommittee reviews the list at least every four years. [e17] A revision of an Other Binding Document follows the process in that document; if it has none, it goes through Section 21 as if it were a Protocol. [e18]
- **How it works #4** (cites e6): Together with the Protocols they make up the scheduling, operating, planning, reliability and Settlement rules that bind Market Participants under PURA section 39.151. [e6]
- **How it works #5** (cites e5, e15, e19): When texts conflict, the order is: Sections 1 through 21 and 24 through 27 prevail over conflicting attachments and Other Binding Documents; an Agreement prevails over the Protocols to the extent of a conflict [e5]; and an Agreement provision that deviates from the Protocols is effective only upon ERCOT Board approval on a showing of good cause. [e15] The Protocols are not intended to govern direct relationships among Market Participants except as they expressly provide. [e19]
- **How it works #7** (cites e24, e23, e25): Capitalized terms take their meaning from Section 2, but a definition in another Section controls in that Section. [e24] "Shall" denotes a duty, "will" a duty unless context says otherwise, "must" a condition precedent or subsequent, "may" a privilege or discretionary power, and "may not" a prohibition. [e23] Times are Central Prevailing Time on a 24-hour clock unless noted. [e25]
- **How it works #9** (cites e9, e27, e11, e12, e10, e13, e28): Changes go through a Nodal Protocol Revision Request. [e9] Parties must use that process before asking the PUCT or another Governmental Authority to make ERCOT change the Protocols, except for good cause shown to the PUCT or other authority. [e27] PRS reviews and recommends action on revision requests, and after a TAC report and impact analysis the ERCOT Board reviews them at its next regularly scheduled meeting. [e11][e12] All revision requests require PUCT approval before implementation. [e10] An Urgent track exists where an existing Protocol or condition is impairing or could imminently impair reliability or market operations, or creates a discrepancy between a settlement formula and the Protocols. [e13] Approved changes are filed with the PUCT for information and posted at least a day before they take effect. [e28] See the nprr topic for the steps.
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e20, e21, e22, e33) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 1.2: Section 1.2 sets ERCOT's role. ERCOT is the central counterparty for transactions it settles, the sole buyer to each seller and sole seller to each buyer of energy, Ancillary Services, RUCs, ERS and other products, except bilateral and self-arranged products. [e20] ERCOT may not profit from its Independent Organization activities and may not use discretion in procuring Ancillary Services or deploying energy to influence, set or control prices. [e21] Nothing in the Protocols transfers control of TSP, DSP or Resource Facilities to ERCOT. [e22] ERCOT must also act to keep non-"public utility" Entities in the ERCOT Region from becoming public utilities under the Federal Power Act. [e33]
- **ERCOT context #3** (cites e26, e14) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 1.8, 4.4: The published Protocol text carries boxes for revisions to apply "upon system implementation". Section 1.8 requires approved provisions not yet implemented to be set out in boxes within the Protocols. [e26] For example, Section 4.4.10 (Credit Requirement for DAM Bids and Offers) of the DAM text contains a box beginning "NPRR1188: Replace paragraph (a) above with the following upon system implementation". [e14] Anything in such a box is pending, not current.
