## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): In the Protocols, a "Resource" means an Energy Storage Resource, a Generation Resource or a Load Resource. Used alone, the term does not include a Settlement Only Generator or an Emergency Response Service Resource. [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e3): - A Generation Resource is a generator capable of providing energy or ancillary service and registered as a Generation Resource. [e3]
- **How it works #2** (cites e2): - An ESR is an energy storage system registered to provide energy and/or ancillary service. [e2]
- **How it works #3** (cites e9, e4): - A Load Resource is a load capable of providing ancillary service and/or energy as demand response, registered as a Load Resource. [e9] A Controllable Load Resource is one that can reduce or increase consumption under ERCOT dispatch control. [e4]
- **How it works #4** (cites e8): - A Settlement Only Generator is settled for exported energy only and may not participate in the ancillary services market, RUC, SCED or make energy offers. [e8]
- **How it works #5** (cites e7): Resource Entities own or control these types. [e7]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e5, e6): Distribution-connected generation has its own split: a Distribution Generation Resource is a Generation Resource connected to the Distribution System, while a Settlement Only Distribution Generator is a generator connected to the Distribution System with a defined rating. [e5][e6] The size tests are in the dgr-sodg topic.
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e10, e11): Pending change: NPRR995 would, upon system implementation, add a Settlement Only Energy Storage System to the list excluded from the bare term "Resource" and would delete the SOG definition (as the saved note states). [e10][e11] This is not yet in effect.
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - "Resource" = ESR, Generation Resource or Load Resource; SOGs and ERS Resources are excluded. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e3, e2): - Generation Resources and ESRs can provide energy and/or ancillary service. [e3][e2]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e4): - A CLR can be dispatched by ERCOT to reduce or increase consumption. [e4]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e8): - SOGs are settlement-only and cannot make energy offers or join SCED. [e8]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e1, e2): (Guidance) "Resource" in the Protocols is narrower than "any generator": a SOG is not a Resource. [e1] A battery can be a Resource (ESR) rather than a Generation Resource. [e2]
- **Questions #2** (cites e3, e8): A Generation Resource can provide energy or ancillary service and is registered as such; a SOG is settled for exported energy only and cannot make energy offers or join SCED, RUC or the ancillary services market. [e3][e8]
- **Questions #4** (cites e4): It can reduce or increase consumption under ERCOT's dispatch control. [e4]
- **Link to resource-entity** (cites e7): related Resource Entity: A Resource Entity owns or controls these Resources. [e7]
- **Link to clr** (cites e4): related CLR (Controllable Load Resource): A CLR is a Load Resource that can be dispatched up or down. [e4]
- **Link to dgr-sodg** (cites e5, e6): related DGR & SODG (Distribution-Connected Generation): A DGR is a Generation Resource connected to the Distribution System; an SODG is a generator connected to the Distribution System with a defined rating. [e5][e6]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e2): related Energy Storage Resource (ESR): ESRs are registered to provide energy and/or ancillary service. [e2]
- **Link to sced** (cites e8): used-in SCED (Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch): SOGs may not participate in SCED, RUC or make energy offers. [e8]
- **Link to ancillary-services** (cites e2, e3, e9): related Ancillary Services (AS): Generation Resources, Load Resources and ESRs can provide ancillary service. [e2][e3][e9]
## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ERCOT sorts market assets into Generation Resources, Load Resources and storage that are dispatched, and settlement-only generators that are just paid for exports. Which bucket you sit in decides what you can offer.
