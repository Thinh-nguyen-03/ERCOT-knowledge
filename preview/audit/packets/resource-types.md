## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to resource-entity** (cites e7): related Resource Entity: A Resource Entity owns or controls these Resources. [e7]
- **Link to clr** (cites e4, e19): related CLR (Controllable Load Resource): A CLR is a Load Resource that can be dispatched up or down, and an ALR is a CLR built from aggregated sites. [e4][e19]
- **Link to dgr-sodg** (cites e14, e16): related DGR & SODG (Distribution-Connected Generation): A DGR is a Generation Resource connected to the Distribution System above ten MW or above one MW if it chooses to register; an SODG is a distribution-connected generator of one MW or less that chooses to register, or above one and up to ten MW that does not register as a DGR. [e14][e16]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e2, e12, e13): related Energy Storage Resource (ESR): ESRs are registered to provide energy and/or ancillary service, and are split into DESRs and TESRs by connection point. [e2][e12][e13]
- **Link to sced** (cites e8): used-in SCED (Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch): SOGs may not participate in SCED, RUC or make energy offers. [e8]
- **Link to ancillary-services** (cites e2, e3, e9): related Ancillary Services (AS): Generation Resources, Load Resources and ESRs can provide ancillary service. [e2][e3][e9]
- **Link to resource-node** (cites e33): related Resource Node: A Resource Entity and ERCOT agree Resource Node parameters in a defined procedure before they enter the Network Operations Model. [e33]
- **Link to large-load-interconnection** (cites e21): related Large Load Interconnection & Batch Zero: A PCLR is a CLR that must keep its registration and qualification until defined exit or de-registration criteria are met. [e21]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ERCOT sorts market assets into Generation Resources, Load Resources and storage that are dispatched, and settlement-only generators that are just paid for exports. Which bucket you sit in decides what you can offer, and the one-MW and ten-MW thresholds decide which bucket small and distribution-connected units fall into.
