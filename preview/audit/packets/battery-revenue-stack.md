## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **How it works #1** (cites e5, e4, e19, e13): Before 2024, batteries participated mainly through ancillary services because they respond quickly. As ESRs saturated several AS markets, they began shifting toward energy arbitrage in 2024. [e5] The IMM expects revenue per unit of capacity to fall as more ESRs enter, because AS markets saturate and arbitrage spreads tighten. [e4] The arbitrage leg works because midday solar pushes real-time prices down, sometimes below zero, so batteries can charge cheaply or even be paid to consume. [e19] Location also matters: the IMM says ESRs can act as both supply and demand, and can often charge at prices below $0 when increasing load at a location resolves congestion. [e13]
- **How it works #2** (cites e46, e9, e15, e44) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2025: Since RTC+B the market sets the stack for each interval. ESRs became a single-model Resource under NPRR1014, replacing the old pairing of a Generation Resource and a Controllable Load Resource. [e46] ERCOT's July 2025 RTC+B battery overview, written before go-live, described the change this way: awards are limited by qualification, physical capability, state of charge, ramp rates and AS duration requirements, and one ESR can receive energy and several AS types in the same SCED execution, where before the QSE had to reserve enough state of charge itself. [e9] The Protocols now in force base AS awards on Resource capability, including State of Charge and SOC limits, and the ASDCs. [e15] An ESR is not eligible for Day-Ahead Make-Whole Payment. [e44]
- **Formula #1** (cites e22, e25) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 8.1, 1.3: Duration caps limit how much of each AS a battery's stored energy can back. Under the Protocols in force (capacity monitoring criteria in Section 8.1.1.3), the capability a QSE shows for a Resource must be limited to what it can sustain for at least the following durations. [e22][e25]
- **Formula #2** (cites e22): - Reg-Up or Reg-Down: 30 minutes. [e22]
- **Formula #3** (cites e23): - RRS (SCED-dispatchable Resources): 30 minutes. [e23]
