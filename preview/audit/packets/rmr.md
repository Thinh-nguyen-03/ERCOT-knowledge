- **Link to ruc** (cites e26): contrasts-with RUC (Reliability Unit Commitment: DRUC/HRUC): RUC is a separate reliability commitment process; a resource whose RMR agreement is approved but not yet executed must stay available for RUC commitment. [e26]

- **Link to market-power-mitigation** (cites e40): related Market Power Mitigation (Competitiveness Tests, Mitigated Offer Caps): The Protocols set the mitigated offer cap for an RMR Unit at the highest value expected to allow SCED to dispatch it. [e40]
- **Link to sced** (cites e38): related SCED (Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch): An RMR Unit committed through DRUC or HRUC can be offered into SCED by ERCOT at the effective Value of Lost Load. [e38]
