## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to negative-prices** (cites e10): causes Negative Prices: The IMM ties offers at or below $0 mainly to wind and solar resources that receive federal production tax credits. [e10]
- **Link to wind-generation** (cites e1, e4): related Wind Generation: Public Law 119-21 ends 45Y and 48E for wind facilities placed in service after 2027, subject to the construction-start effective date. [e1][e4]
- **Link to solar-generation** (cites e1, e14, e2): related Solar Generation: The same 2027 placed-in-service cutoff applies to solar facilities, which the law lists as an applicable facility alongside wind. [e1][e14][e2]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e3, e18): contrasts-with Energy Storage Resource (ESR): The 48E wind and solar termination does not apply to energy storage placed in service at those facilities, but storage faces its own, higher foreign-content thresholds. [e3][e18]
- **Link to ppa-vppa** (cites e7): related PPA & Virtual PPA: A REC can trade separately from energy, so renewable attributes can be bought apart from the power. [e7]
- **Link to ercot** (cites e6, e8) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 39.9113: operates ERCOT: ERCOT administers the REC Trading Program, and PURA 39.9113 has the ERCOT-region independent organization keep an accreditation and banking system. [e6][e8]
- **Link to puct** (cites e28, e36): regulates PUCT (Public Utility Commission of Texas): PUCT rule 25.173 sets the REC program terms; the PUCT appointed ERCOT as program administrator in 2000 and reaffirmed it on November 30, 2023. [e28][e36]
- **Link to texas-legislature** (cites e9, e25, e26) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2023: regulates Texas Legislature: HB 1500 (2023) repealed the PURA 39.904 renewable goal and required a solar-only program that ended September 1, 2025. [e9][e25][e26]
- **Link to resource-mix** (cites e48): affects ERCOT Resource Mix: As of October 31, 2026 ERCOT was tracking 151,798 MW of solar, 49,781 MW of wind and 160,102 MW of battery interconnection requests. [e48]
- **Link to merit-order** (cites e45, e10): affects Merit Order / Supply Stack: The IMM reports that the share of capacity offered at or below zero, mostly tax-credit-supported wind and solar, rose from roughly a quarter (offered below zero) in 2022 to 35% (at or below $0) in 2025. [e45][e10]
