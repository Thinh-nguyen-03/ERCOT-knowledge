- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e5, e7): Winter Storm Uri was the February 2021 cold weather event in which, from February 8 through 20, 1,045 generating units in the event area had 4,124 outages, derates or failures to start. [e1] In ERCOT it led to firm load shed that began early on February 15, with 20,000 MW ordered by 7:00 p.m. that day. [e5][e7]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e1, e3): The federal staff report names two causes, both triggered by cold weather: generating units unprepared for cold weather failed in large numbers, and the natural gas fuel supply struggled to meet both residential heating load and generating unit demand. [e2] Across the whole event area, 75 percent of the 4,124 outages, derates and failures to start were caused by freezing issues (44.2 percent) or fuel issues (31.4 percent). [e1] The UT Austin report adds that natural gas production, storage and distribution facilities did not deliver the full amount of fuel that gas power plants demanded. [e3]
- **How it works #2** (cites e4, e5, e6): At the worst point ERCOT reported 52,277 MW out of 107,514 MW of installed capacity unavailable, or 48.6%. [e4] ERCOT moved through EEA 1, EEA 2 and EEA 3 in the first hour and twenty minutes of February 15 and began firm load shed at 01:20. [e5] Frequency dropped below the 59.4 Hz generator underfrequency trip level at 1:51 a.m.; the FERC-NERC team says that if those relays had tripped, about 17,000 MW of generation would have been lost, potentially causing a total blackout of the ERCOT footprint. [e6]
- **How it works #3** (cites e7, e10, e5): By 7:00 p.m. on February 15, ERCOT had ordered 20,000 MW of firm load shed and held it for nearly three days. [e7] The last firm load shed orders were cancelled at 12:42 a.m. on February 18. [e10] ERCOT returned to normal operations on Friday, February 19. [e5]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e11, e12, e14, e15): Prices: the UT report says the price of electricity went to $9,000 per MWh and stayed there by PUCT order, with some market price-setting rules suspended during the blackouts. [e11] Gas prices at Texas trading hubs went from normally much less than $10/MMBtu to over $400/MMBtu. [e12] The peaker net margin threshold was exceeded on February 16, 2021, the only time it has happened in ERCOT's history.
