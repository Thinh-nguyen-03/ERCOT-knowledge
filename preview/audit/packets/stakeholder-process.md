## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e3, e4, e5, e6, e7): The stakeholder process is the committee path that ERCOT Protocol changes follow: the Protocol Revision Subcommittee (PRS), then the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC), then the ERCOT Board, then the PUCT. [e3][e4][e5][e6] PURA requires ERCOT's governing body to maintain a formal process for adopting new or revised protocols as a condition of keeping its certification. [e7]
- **How it works #1** (cites e12, e1, e2, e3): PRS reviews and recommends action on formally submitted revision requests. [e12] Its meetings are open to ERCOT, ERCOT Members, Market Participants, the Reliability Monitor, the NERC Regional Entity, the IMM and PUCT Staff. [e1] Each Market Segment may participate and each has equal voting power. [e2] PRS can send a request to working groups or TAC subcommittees for expertise, but PRS keeps ultimate responsibility for processing it. [e3]
- **How it works #2** (cites e4, e5, e6): TAC takes up requests that PRS has sent forward. It can recommend approval as recommended or as modified, reject, table, remand to PRS with instructions, or refer to another TAC subcommittee or working group. [e4] The ERCOT Board then reviews the TAC Report and the Impact Analysis at its next regularly scheduled meeting. [e5] Every revision request then needs PUCT approval before implementation. [e6]
- **How it works #3** (cites e9): PRS decisions can be challenged. Any ERCOT Member, Market Participant, PUCT Staff, the Reliability Monitor, the IMM, the NERC Regional Entity or ERCOT may appeal a PRS decision to reject, table or refer a request directly to TAC. [e9]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e10, e11, e3, e4): Other subcommittees appear in the Protocols' acronym list: the Wholesale Market Subcommittee (WMS) and the Reliability and Operations Subcommittee (ROS). [e10][e11] The saved Protocol text for the revision process (Section 21) names PRS and TAC as the committees on the NPRR path, and it treats other TAC subcommittees as places PRS or TAC can refer a request. [e3][e4]
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e8): The top of the path is shaped by statute. ERCOT board members are chosen by a selection committee made up of one appointee each from the governor, the lieutenant governor and the speaker of the house. [e8]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e3, e4, e5, e6): - Path for an NPRR: PRS, then TAC, then ERCOT Board, then PUCT. [e3][e4][e5][e6]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e2): - Each Market Segment has equal voting power at PRS. [e2]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e4): - TAC can recommend, reject, table, remand to PRS, or refer to another subcommittee. [e4]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e6): - PUCT approval is required for every revision request before implementation. [e6]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e7): - PURA conditions ERCOT's certification on a formal protocol-adoption process. [e7]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e5, e6, e3, e10, e11): (Guidance) A TAC vote is not the end of the line. TAC only recommends, and the Board and then the PUCT must act before a change is implemented. [e5][e6] Also, WMS and ROS are subcommittees listed in the Protocols' acronyms, but the saved text does not put them on the standard NPRR route; PRS is the subcommittee that reviews NPRRs. [e3][e10][e11]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) Protocol changes in ERCOT are worked out in open stakeholder committees, PRS first and TAC second, and then go to the ERCOT Board and the PUCT. The committees recommend; the PUCT has the final say.
