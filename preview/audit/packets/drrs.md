## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e7, e11, e12, e2) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2026: DRRS is a new Ancillary Service, set up under NPRR1309 (approved by the PUCT on July 9, 2026 but not yet implemented in the saved sources), that provides operating reserves to manage uncertainty on the ERCOT System while mitigating the need for Reliability Unit Commitment (RUC) instructions. [e1][e7][e11][e12] It exists because state law requires ERCOT to procure dispatchable reliability reserve services on a day-ahead and real-time basis to account for market uncertainty. [e2]
- **How it works #1** (cites e3): The statute sets the minimum design. A participating resource must be able to run for at least four hours at its high sustained limit, be online and dispatchable within two hours of being called, and have the flexibility to handle inter-hour operational problems. ERCOT must also reduce reliability unit commitment by the amount of DRRS it procures. [e3]
- **How it works #2** (cites e4, e7): Under the approved NPRR1309 language, DRRS comes from both Off-Line and On-Line Generation Resources that can ramp to a set output within two hours and hold it for at least four consecutive hours. [e4] Compared with the withdrawn NPRR1235, which allowed only Off-Line Resources, NPRR1309 lets DRRS be awarded in Real-Time, so its procurement can be co-optimized with energy and the other Ancillary Services. [e7]
- **How it works #3** (cites e16, e5, e4, e13) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2026: NPRR1309 also changes Non-Spin: its revision description says that, given DRRS has at least a four-hour duration, the Non-Spin duration requirements are reduced from four hours to two hours. [e16] The IMM describes this as taking effect upon DRRS implementation, and says DRRS is statutorily required to carry a four-hour duration requirement. [e5] Storage participation is not in NPRR1309; ERCOT filed NPRR1340 and NOGRR288 in June 2026 to continue that work. [e4][e13]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e11, e10, e12) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2026: Status as of the saved sources: NPRR1309 is decided but not yet live. The PUCT approved it on July 9, 2026, after the ERCOT Board recommended approval on June 2, 2026. [e11][e10] ERCOT's impact analysis estimated a project duration of 20 to 30 months. [e12] The saved sources do not give a go-live date.
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e6, e15, e7, e9, e14): DRRS took three tries. NPRR1203 (2023) proposed it as a subtype of Non-Spin to meet HB 1500's December 1, 2024 deadline. [e6] NPRR1235 (May 2024) proposed it as a stand-alone Ancillary Service but Off-Line only; ERCOT withdrew it and filed NPRR1309 (November 2025) instead. [e15][e7] The design was contested: TAC endorsed it with five opposing votes from cooperatives and independent generators. [e9] The IMM, in its 2025 report, said NPRR1309 follows both of its design principles and, if implemented correctly, should reduce costly out-of-market actions. [e14]
