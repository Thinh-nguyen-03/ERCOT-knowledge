## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Scenarios) - (Reasoning) Then give a view: a spike is likely if the net load ramp is steep, batteries reach the net peak with little stored energy, and Non-Spin or ECRS look short. It is less likely if wind picks up in the evening.
- (Say it like this) (Guidance) Evening prices spike because solar drops off faster than demand does. The last megawatts come from fast gas and batteries that are running low on stored energy. Since RTC, any reserve shortage is priced through the AS demand curves, and that pushes energy prices up in the same dispatch run.
