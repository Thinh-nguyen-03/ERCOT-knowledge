## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) ERCOT sells CRRs in a monthly auction and in long-term sequences up to three years out, each time offering only a share of what the network can support, and it prices them at the shadow price of the constraint. Revenue goes back to load, and day-ahead congestion rent funds what CRR holders are paid.
