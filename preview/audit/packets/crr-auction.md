## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): The CRR Auction sells the available network capacity of the ERCOT transmission system that has not been allocated as PCRRs or sold in a previous auction, and lets CRR Owners offer CRRs they already hold. [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e11, e3, e5): Two kinds of auction run. The CRR Monthly Auction is held once per month and sells one-month PTP Options and PTP Obligations for the following month. [e2] Twice a year a Long-Term Auction Sequence runs six successive auctions, starting with the next six-month period (January-June or July-December) and then the five periods after it. [e11][e3] Each auction is a single-round, simultaneous auction across all products. [e5]
- **How it works #2** (cites e4, e6, e7): Only part of the network is sold in each auction. Auction capacity is 90% for the Monthly Auction, and for the Long-Term sequence 70%, 55%, 40%, 30%, 20% and 10% for the first through sixth six-month windows. [e4][e6] Awards must pass the Simultaneous Feasibility Test, which confirms the system can support the awarded CRRs under normal conditions while observing security constraints. [e7]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e9, e10, e8): The IMM says participants can buy CRRs in monthly blocks up to three years ahead to manage congestion risk. [e9] The IMM says ERCOT distributes auction revenue to Load Serving Entities through CARD, by system-wide load ratio share for cross-zone CRRs and zonal share for same-zone CRRs. [e10] The IMM reports auction revenue up almost 6% in 2025, after 14% to 32% annual growth in 2021-2024. [e8]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e2): - Monthly Auction: held monthly, sells one-month terms for the next month. [e2]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e11, e3): - Long-Term Auction Sequence: twice a year, six successive auctions of six-month terms. [e11][e3]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e4, e6): - Auction capacity: 90% monthly; 70% down to 10% across the Long-Term windows. [e4][e6]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e5): - Single-round, simultaneous auction. [e5]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e8): - 2025 auction revenue growth: almost 6%. [e8]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e1, e12, e7): The auction is not the only source of CRRs: PCRRs are allocated outside it and PTP Options and PTP Obligations (other than those with Refund) can also be traded bilaterally. [e1][e12] The SFT is a market feasibility test that confirms the system can support the awarded CRRs. [e7]
- **Questions #2** (cites e6): The percentage falls with each successive six-month window sold, from 70% for the first to 10% for the sixth, so less of the network is offered for the periods furthest out. [e6]
- **Questions #4** (cites e10): Load Serving Entities, through CARD (as the IMM describes it): cross-zone revenue by ERCOT-wide load ratio share and same-zone revenue by zonal share. [e10]
- **Questions #6** (cites e7): The Simultaneous Feasibility Test on the awarded set of CRRs. [e7]
- **Link to crr** (cites e1): prerequisite-of CRR (Congestion Revenue Right): The CRR topic covers the instruments, settlement and the distribution of auction revenue; this topic covers how the auction runs. [e1]
- **Link to pcrr** (cites e1): related PCRR (Pre-Assigned CRR): Auction capacity excludes what is allocated as PCRRs. [e1]
- **Link to ptp-obligation** (cites e2, e11): related PTP Obligation: The Monthly Auction and Long-Term Auction Sequence sell PTP Options and PTP Obligations. [e2][e11]
