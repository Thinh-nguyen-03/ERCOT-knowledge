## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) Wind in ERCOT is a lot of energy but not much of it lines up with the afternoon peak; it does show up in the evening, which is why its capacity factor at the net peak is higher than solar's. Its volatility, congestion-driven curtailment and forecast error are the things to model.
