## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e2, e3, e10, e9) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2021: NERC is the North American Electric Reliability Corporation, certified by FERC as the Electric Reliability Organization (ERO) responsible for establishing mandatory Reliability Standards, which FERC must approve. [e1] Texas RE (Texas Reliability Entity; the 2021 report also calls it Texas Regional Entity) is one of the Regional Entities, which are independent regional bodies with delegated authority from NERC to propose and enforce Reliability Standards. [e2][e3][e10] The Protocols define the NERC Regional Entity as an Entity with delegated authority from NERC, approved by FERC, to propose and enforce NERC Reliability Standards in the ERCOT Region. [e9]
- **How it works #1** (cites e1, e2, e11) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2024: NERC is responsible for establishing the mandatory Reliability Standards, and Regional Entities have delegated authority from NERC to propose and enforce them. [e1][e2] A January 2024 FERC staff primer says the standards, once FERC approves them, must be met by the applicable industry participants designated in each standard, so the grid is planned and operated to meet them.
