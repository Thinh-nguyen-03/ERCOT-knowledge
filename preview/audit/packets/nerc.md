## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) NERC sets mandatory reliability standards under FERC oversight, and Texas RE is the regional entity that, with ERCOT, has assessed generator winter readiness. This is separate from the PUCT's market and rules role.
