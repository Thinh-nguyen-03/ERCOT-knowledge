## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e10): The Independent Market Monitor (IMM) is the entity selected to monitor ERCOT's wholesale electric market under PURA §39.1515 and PUCT rule 25.365. [e1] Potomac Economics fills this role and files the annual State of the Market report with the PUCT. [e10]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2): ERCOT must contract with an entity the PUCT selects to act as the commission's wholesale electric market monitor. The monitor's statutory jobs are to detect and prevent market manipulation, recommend ways to make the wholesale market more efficient, and give independent analysis of material proposed market changes. The PUCT may not stop the monitor from speaking to or advising the Legislature. [e2]
- **How it works #2** (cites e3, e5): The PUCT rule sets two objectives: detect and prevent manipulation and market power abuse, and evaluate the market's operations, current rules and proposed rule changes, with recommendations to improve efficiency. [e3] To do this, the IMM can require ERCOT and market participants to submit any information it considers necessary, and they must respond completely, accurately and on time. [e5]
- **How it works #3** (cites e4, e8, e15): The IMM has no enforcement power. Its authority covers monitoring, analysis and reporting. [e4] It must report all potential market manipulation and all discovered or potential rule violations in writing, directly to the commission and its staff. [e8] The commission retains all enforcement authority. [e15]
- **How it works #4** (cites e9): Its required reporting includes an annual State of the Market report. That report assesses competitiveness, how efficiently ERCOT manages the balancing energy, ancillary service and congestion rights markets, and how effective its congestion management is. It also looks for flaws, inefficiencies and manipulation opportunities in the market design, and recommends improvements. [e9]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e6, e7): The IMM's independence is structural. It is an office independent from ERCOT and is not supervised by ERCOT in its monitoring and investigations, even though ERCOT contracts for it [e6] and funds it from the fee authorized under PURA §39.151. [e7]
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e13): The IMM also has a formal role in resource adequacy. If ERCOT's assessment shows a reviewed system failing the reliability standard, the IMM must independently review ERCOT's proposed market design changes and their expected costs. [e13]
- **ERCOT context #3** (cites e11, e12, e14): In its 2025 report the IMM found the share of top-quartile net load hours with a pivotal supplier had fallen from over 90% in 2022 to 35% in 2025. [e11] Even so, it again recommended system-wide market power mitigation, warning that load growth could make the market structurally uncompetitive at high load within a few years. [e12] It also argued that the NPRR 1296 residential demand response proposal would undermine ERCOT's energy-only market and its price signals. [e14]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) The IMM, Potomac Economics, is the PUCT's independent market watchdog. It can demand information from ERCOT and participants, but it cannot penalize anyone: it reports to the commission, which enforces. Its State of the Market report is the best single read on how the market performed and where the monitor thinks the design is broken.
