- (Say it like this) (Guidance) "Getting connected is now a batch process with real money up front and a pause on new data center energization while ERCOT audits Batch Zero, and the data center should expect curtailment obligations in emergencies and possibly only a partial allocation of what it asked for. On the supply side, a solar VPPA is a financial swap, not power: the site still buys at its load zone price around the clock, so the VPPA hedges only the hours and the location it covers, and the transmission bill is a separate line that may move to a 20-year minimum billing demand."
