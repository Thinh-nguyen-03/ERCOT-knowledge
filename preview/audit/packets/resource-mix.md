## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) Gas still produces the largest quoted share of ERCOT energy, but most new capacity is solar and batteries, and thermal capacity has been roughly flat for a decade, so the question is shifting from "how much capacity" to "what is available after sunset." That is why net load, storage duration and gas units' evening role matter more each year.
