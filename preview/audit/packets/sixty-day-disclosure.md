- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e3, e27, e8, e6, e7): The rule has two tiers. Aggregated information, such as supply curves, actual output and actual load, is posted two calendar days after the day. [e2] Entity-specific information, with resource-specific offer information linked to the resource, submitting entity and controlling entity names, is posted 60 calendar days after. [e3] Where a resource has multiple offers, ERCOT posts the information for each offer with the submitting and controlling entity names. [e27] The Protocols mirror this: information derived from each SCED execution for the ERCOT System and each Disclosure Area is posted two days after the Operating Day, and resource-level SCED data and DAM data are posted 60 days prior to the current Operating Day.
