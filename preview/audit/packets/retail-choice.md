## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to rep** (cites e3): related REP (Retail Electric Provider): Customers may choose a retail electric provider, or a cooperative or municipal utility that offers choice. [e3]
- **Link to tdsp** (cites e4, e5): related TDSP (Transmission & Distribution Service Provider): The wires utility cannot sell electricity and, with an exception for IDR-metered commercial and industrial customers, its affiliate continues to provide metering after choice begins. [e4][e5]
- **Link to noe** (cites e2, e14): contrasts-with NOIE (Non-Opt-In Entity: Munis & Co-ops): Co-ops and municipal utilities are outside customer choice unless they opt in, and an MOU's opt-in resolution is irrevocable. [e2][e14]
- **Link to esi-id** (cites e10): related ESI ID: A Switch Request must include an ESI ID. [e10]
- **Link to tx-set** (cites e8): used-in TX SET (Texas Standard Electronic Transactions): A CR submits a Switch Request to ERCOT using the 814_01 transaction. [e8]
- **Link to mass-transition** (cites e11): related Mass Transition: ERCOT initiates a Mass Transition of an LSE's ESI IDs if the LSE serving Load fails to cure a QSE Affiliation Breach. [e11]
- **Link to polr** (cites e20): related POLR (Provider of Last Resort): A cooperative that initiates choice must designate itself or another entity as provider of last resort. [e20]
- **Link to lse** (cites e22): related LSE (Load Serving Entity): Each MOU and EC must register with ERCOT regardless of whether it plans to be a NOIE or a Competitive Retailer. [e22]
