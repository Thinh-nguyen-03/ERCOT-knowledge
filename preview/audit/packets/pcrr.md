## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) PCRRs are legacy congestion hedges given to co-ops and municipal utilities for plants and contracts that predate the market. They are priced as a percentage of the auction clearing price, and for eligible non-solid-fuel, non-combined-cycle Resources the NOIE chooses between a refund option and a capacity option, except where the Protocols require the capacity option.
