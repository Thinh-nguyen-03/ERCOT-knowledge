## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **How it works #1** (cites e3, e13, e14, e12): **Who and what qualifies.** A NOIE is an electric cooperative or municipally owned utility that does not offer Customer Choice. [e3] A NOIE may designate an agent to manage its PCRRs, but ERCOT's relationship is with the NOIE, and ERCOT relies exclusively on documentation provided or confirmed by the NOIE to determine continued eligibility. [e13] ERCOT publishes a list of eligible NOIEs including each one's ownership or contractual capacity from pre-September 1, 1999 Resources. [e14] An existing Generation Resource that interconnected on or after June 1, 2021 under paragraph (3) of Section 1.6.5 cannot qualify as a pre-September 1, 1999 Resource for PCRR purposes. [e12]
- **How it works #2** (cites e15, e16, e17, e4, e18, e19): For an owned Resource, the NOIE must have owned it before September 1, 1999 with uninterrupted ownership, the Resource must have stayed in service except for Maintenance, Forced, Opportunity or Planned Outages (including a Mothballed Resource operating under a Seasonal Operation Period), and the NOIE must use it to meet service obligations in its territory at least equal to the nominated PCRR amount. [e15] For a contract, the term must exceed five years (evergreen clauses count only if in place before September 1, 1999), and the NOIE must be entitled to capacity from specific Resources: contracts not backed by specific Resources are not eligible. [e16][e17] For a portfolio supply contract that does not describe each NOIE's entitlement by Resource, each NOIE is allocated by its 2003 4-CP ratio share, calculated as its 4-CP divided by the total 4-CP of all NOIEs supplied by the portfolio in 2003, multiplied by the 2010 registration-data net max sustainable rating of each eligible Resource. [e4][e18] The DC Tie is treated as a PCRR-eligible Resource for the contracts of Tex-La Electric Cooperative of Texas, Inc. with supply resources outside ERCOT that meet the requirements of paragraphs (b)(i)-(ii) and (iv)-(v) of the eligibility criteria. [e19]
- **How it works #3** (cites e6, e9, e30, e5): **Limits on the amount.** Amounts are capped by the Seasonal net max sustainable rating of eligible Resources and never above the 2010 registration-data rating. [e6] A repowered unit is limited to the original turbine/generator sets. [e9] Nominations are based on forecasted peak Demand, subject to the Resource capacity limit. [e30] A NOIE serving several Load Zones nominates per zone, by contract rights or in proportion to peak Load served. [e5]
- **How it works #4** (cites e20, e21, e22, e57): **Outages and mothballing.** A NOIE keeps eligibility for a Resource out of service more than 12 consecutive months under a Forced Outage if it demonstrates the Outage continues to be necessary and does not require the Resource Entity to cease or suspend operation. [e20] In a Nomination Year in which such a Resource is expected to return, it is eligible only for months it is expected to be in service for the entire month. [e21] A Mothballed Generation Resource also keeps eligibility, whether or not ERCOT determines it is necessary for RMR Service. [e22] However, the NOIE does not have an exclusive right to retain allocated PCRRs while the Resource is designated Mothballed. [e57]
- **How it works #5** (cites e24, e23, e25, e26, e27, e28, e29): **Disqualification.** PCRRs for a Resource are lost if it is retired and decommissioned, or if the NOIE sells it, including by selling its energy or capacity by contract; a transfer to an Affiliate or to a generation and transmission cooperative for the NOIE's benefit is not disqualifying if all other requirements remain. [e24][e23] Any change after September 1, 1999 in the Resources designated to back a long-term contract disqualifies it. [e25] A NOIE is no longer eligible after it opts into competition, except South Texas Electric Cooperative, which may be eligible for up to three years after entering competition. [e26] When a disqualifying event occurs, the PCRRs for the Resource are voided from the date of the event and future allocations end. [e27] If it occurs during the term of allocated PCRRs and the NOIE still holds the CRRs, they are either voided with a refund of the discounted purchase price, or retained with the NOIE paying the price differential to the full auction price; refund-option PCRRs (allocated at no charge) are voided with no financial exchange. [e28][e29]
- **How it works #6** (cites e8, e10, e42, e33, e31, e34, e43): **Nomination and allocation.** A PCRR nomination is a request for one-month strips of a NOIE-specified CRR type for each month of the PCRR Nomination Year, the third calendar year after the nomination process. [e8][e10] The NOIE must specify PTP Option, PTP Obligation or a combination before allocation, and may not change it afterwards. [e42] Nominations are due at least 30 Business Days before the first Long-Term Auction Sequence of the year, ERCOT allocates at least 25 Business Days before it, and there is no PCRR nomination for a second sequence in the year. [e33] ERCOT tests nominations with the SFT using 100% of each month's expected network topology; if infeasible, it proportionately reduces requests by their Impact Ratio on violated constraints. [e31] Before each Monthly Auction, ERCOT re-evaluates any reduced nominations for the month and allocates more, if feasible, up to the original amount. [e34] PCRRs cannot be bilaterally traded through ERCOT systems before completion of the auction used to determine their value. [e43]
- **How it works #7** (cites e35, e36, e37, e38, e39): **Refund versus capacity option.** For eligible non-solid-fuel and non-combined-cycle Resources the NOIE must choose before allocation either the refund option or the capacity option, for every month of the allocation year, not a combination. [e35] A NOIE or group with 2003 peak Load above 2,300 MW must use the capacity option for those Resources. [e36] Under the refund option the NOIE refunds congestion revenues received above those flowing to it for its Output Schedule of the Resource at the PCRR source, and such PCRRs are not transferable. [e37][e38] Under the capacity option the NOIE may nominate up to 100% of the lesser of net unit capacity or contractual amount at a capacity factor no greater than 40% over each calendar year. [e39]
- **How it works #8** (cites e32, e40, e11, e41): **Price.** The price a NOIE pays for a PCRR allocated in the initial nomination process is based on the corresponding CRR clearing price in the CRR First Offering. [e32] After the allocation process and subsequent auction, PCRRs (other than refund-option PCRRs for Resources other than solid-fuel and combined-cycle Resources, which are at no charge) are priced as a percentage of the auction clearing price: PTP Options at 10% (nuclear, coal, lignite, combined-cycle), 15% (gas steam) or 20% (hydro, wind, simple cycle and others). [e40][e11] PTP Obligations are at 5%, 7.5% or 10% for the same classes if the clearing price is positive and 100% if it is negative. [e41]
- **Formula #1** (cites e53, e45, e41, e44, e54, e55, e56, e46): For PCRR PTP Obligations the charge per source-sink pair for the TOU period is: if the auction clearing price OBLPR is positive, PCRROBLAMT = PCRROBLF x OBLPR x PCRROBL; otherwise PCRROBLAMT = OBLPR x PCRROBL. [e53] PCRROBLF is the pricing factor for pre-allocated PTP Obligations by Resource technology, set in Section 7.4.2.2(1)(g)(ii) (the 5%, 7.5% and 10% figures above). [e45][e41] For PTP Options the charge is PCRROPTAMT = PCRROPTF x OPTPR x PCRROPT, where PCRROPTF is the pricing factor for pre-allocated PTP Options by Resource technology (Section 7.4.2.2(1)(g)(i)), OPTPR is the clearing price of the PTP Option in that auction and PCRROPT is the MW quantity of the holder's PTP Options allocated before the auction. [e44][e54][e55][e56] The Protocols say ERCOT charges each CRR Account Holder for pre-assigned PTP Obligations allocated before each auction. [e46]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e52, e7, e50, e49, e51, e47, e48): The IMM says ERCOT allocates PCRRs to NOIEs based on generation ownership or contractual arrangements that predate retail competition, and that holders pay only a fraction of the auction value of comparable CRRs. [e52][e7] The IMM measures the discount as the difference between the auction value and the value charged to the purchaser. [e50] It reported a total PCRR discount of $76 million in 2021 and $84 million in 2022, and said it decreased slightly to $76 million in 2023. [e49][e51] ERCOT's CRR handbook says ERCOT works with NOIEs at least annually to confirm and validate eligibility, and describes a typical annual cycle in which the nomination window opens 10 days after the review period and lasts 5 days. [e47][e48]
