## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e14, e15, e12): ERCOT issues three levels of communication in anticipation of a possible Emergency Condition, in rising order: the Operating Condition Notice (OCN), the Advisory and the Watch. [e1][e14][e15] The Emergency Notice is the communication issued to declare that ERCOT is operating in an Emergency Condition. [e12]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e13): The type of communication depends mainly on how much time the market has to respond before an Emergency Condition. Lead times range from days to immediate, and ERCOT may skip steps if there is not enough time. [e2] Each communication must specify severity, the area affected, areas potentially affected and the anticipated duration. [e13]
- **How it works #2** (cites e3, e4, e7, e5, e11, e6): An OCN is informational only and gives ERCOT no extra operational authority; its reasons include unplanned transmission outages, insufficient resources for forecast conditions and weather concerns. [e3][e4] An Advisory is issued, for example, when more ancillary services will be needed or weather requires more lead time than the DAM allows; ERCOT notifies TSPs and QSEs, and QSEs notify resources and LSEs. [e7][e5] A Watch is issued when, for example, SCED's market-based congestion management will not resolve transmission security violations or forced outages require operating with active security violations. [e11] ERCOT posts it on its website and by hotline, and each QSE must immediately notify the Market Participants it represents. [e6]
- **How it works #3** (cites e8): An Emergency Notice is issued when ERCOT cannot maintain minimum reliability standards with every practicably obtainable resource, or cannot take immediate action to relieve a Transmission Element above its Emergency Rating. [e8]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e10, e9): The notices tie to resource obligations: when ERCOT issues any of the four due to forecast or actual cold or hot weather, QSEs must update the COP, telemetry and outage or derate reporting for each generation resource and ESR to reflect operating limits. [e10] ERCOT uses PRC and system frequency to determine the appropriate Emergency Notice and EEA levels. [e9]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1, e14, e15, e12): - Four communications: OCN, Advisory, Watch (three levels in anticipation) and Emergency Notice. [e1][e14][e15][e12]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e3): - An OCN is for information only; ERCOT exercises no additional operational authority with it. [e3]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e2): - ERCOT can bypass steps when time is short. [e2]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e6): - A Watch is posted to the website and by TO and QSE hotline. [e6]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e10): - Weather-driven notices require QSEs to update COP, telemetry and derate reporting for resources. [e10]
- **Say it like this #1** (cites e3, e7, e11, e12, e2): ERCOT escalates in steps: an OCN is a heads-up, an Advisory says more reserves or lead time are needed, a Watch says normal market tools may not solve a security problem, and an Emergency Notice says ERCOT is in an Emergency Condition. It can skip steps if time is short. [e3][e7][e11][e12][e2]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e17, e9, e15): An Emergency Notice is not an EEA level. The EEA is the orderly procedure for maximizing resources and, only if necessary, curtailing load. [e17] ERCOT uses PRC and frequency to pick both the Emergency Notice and the EEA level. [e9] A Watch is also not an EEA; it is the third of the three pre-emergency communications. [e15]
- **Questions #2** (cites e1, e14, e15, e12): OCN, Advisory and Watch, then the Emergency Notice when ERCOT is operating in an Emergency Condition. [e1][e14][e15][e12]
- **Questions #4** (cites e3): No. OCNs are informational only and ERCOT exercises no additional operational authority, though it may ask QSEs for more information. [e3]
- **Questions #6** (cites e10, e5, e6): Update the COP, telemetry and outage or derate reporting for each generation resource and ESR to reflect operating limits, and for Advisories and Watches pass the notice to those they represent. [e10][e5][e6]
