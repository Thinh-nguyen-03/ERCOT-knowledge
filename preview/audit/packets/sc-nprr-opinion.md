## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to nprr** (cites e1, e5): example-of NPRR (Nodal Protocol Revision Request): An NPRR is the request to add, edit or delete Protocol language, and every Revision Request needs PUCT approval before implementation. [e1][e5]
- **Link to stakeholder-process** (cites e10, e14, e15): used-in Stakeholder Process (TAC, PRS, WMS, ROS, Board): NPRR1309 went through PRS, TAC, the ERCOT Board and the PUCT, with recorded segment votes. [e10][e14][e15]
- **Link to drrs** (cites e8): example-of DRRS (Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service): Under NPRR1309, DRRS is offered, awarded and paid in both the DAM and the RTM. [e8]
- **Link to non-spin** (cites e9, e26): affects Non-Spinning Reserve: NPRR1309, approved with an effective date of "Upon system implementation", reduces the Non-Spin duration requirements from four hours to two hours, given that DRRS has at least a four-hour duration. [e9][e26]
- **Link to imm** (cites e17, e18): related Independent Market Monitor (Potomac Economics): The IMM recommends a one-hour NSRS duration and argues ERCOT's AS Methodology over-procures reserves. [e17][e18]
- **Link to four-cp** (cites e19, e20): related 4CP Transmission Charges: The IMM says 4CP no longer aligns with cost-causation principles, and a PUCT proposal would replace 4CP with 12CP. [e19][e20]
- **Link to load-zone** (cites e21): related Load Zone: The IMM keeps recommending that ERCOT reconfigure its load zones to reflect current congestion patterns. [e21]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e17): affects Energy Storage Resource (ESR): A four-hour NSRS duration means a storage resource must hold four hours of energy for its award, which the IMM says raises its cost of participating. [e17]
- **Link to asdc** (cites e31): affects ASDC (Ancillary Service Demand Curves): The joint ERCOT/IMM proposal replaced the original linear DRRS demand curve with one derived from the Aggregate Operating Reserve Demand Curve. [e31]
- **Link to ruc** (cites e6, e42): affects RUC (Reliability Unit Commitment: DRUC/HRUC): The statute requires DRRS to reduce the amount of RUC by the amount of DRRS procured, and RUC will prioritize committing Off-Line DRRS Resources. [e6][e42]
