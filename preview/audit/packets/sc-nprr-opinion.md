## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e5): This scenario asks the candidate to take and defend a position on an ERCOT market rule, which in practice means a Revision Request. An NPRR is a request to add, edit, delete, revise or clarify the Protocols, and every Revision Request needs PUCT approval before it is implemented. [e1][e5]
- **How it works #1** (cites e4, e2, e3, e5): Step 1, know the path. To receive consideration, comments on a posted Revision Request must be delivered within 14 days of posting, PRS reviews it, and TAC can recommend approval, reject, table, remand or refer it. [e4][e2] The ERCOT Board can designate a request a Board Priority Revision Request, and the PUCT must approve before implementation. [e3][e5]
- **How it works #2** (cites e7, e6, e8, e9, e26, e16): Step 2, pick a live debate with a public record. ERCOT called NPRR1309 the third iteration of the DRRS design, and the statute sets minimum characteristics for DRRS: reduce RUC by the amount procured, run at least four hours at High Sustained Limit, and be On-Line within two hours of being called. [e7][e6] DRRS is offered, awarded and paid in both the DAM and RTM. [e7][e8] The contested piece was a side change: under the approved NPRR, Non-Spin's duration requirement is reduced from four hours to two. [e9] The PUCT Report lists the NPRR's effective date as "Upon system implementation", with an estimated project duration of 20 to 30 months, so the change is approved but tied to system implementation. [e26][e16]
- **How it works #3** (cites e22, e23, e25, e10, e11, e12, e14, e15): Step 3, give the positions as recorded. At PRS on 4/15/26 participants proposed desktop edits to maintain the current four-hour Non-Spin duration. [e22] The IMM opposed that PRS version and proposed a two-hour Non-Spin; ERCOT's 4/28/26 comments did the same, and on 4/29/26 TAC voted to recommend approval with five opposing votes from LCRA, Brazos, STEC, Vistra and Constellation. [e23][e25][e10] LCRA called the two-hour change premature without more analysis, and Vistra argued it was not required for DRRS, should wait for the 2029 AS methodology review, and could degrade durable reserves and increase RUC of thermal units. [e11][e12] The Board recommended approval unanimously on 6/2/26 and the PUCT approved on 7/9/26. [e14][e15]
- **How it works #4** (cites e17, e18) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2025: Step 4, bring evidence for your side. The IMM says NSRS was priced at a premium in 2025 because of oversized procurement and the four-hour duration, under which a storage resource must carry four hours of energy for its NSRS award; its Recommendation 2024-2 asks for one hour. [e17] It also says ERCOT's proposed AS Methodology procures 140% more than the 1-in-10 load shed standard requires. [e18]
