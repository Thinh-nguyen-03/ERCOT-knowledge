## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to crr-auction** (cites e1): used-in CRR Auctions: CRRs are auctioned in 5x16, 2x16 and 7x8 TOU blocks. [e1]
- **Link to crr** (cites e2): used-in CRR (Congestion Revenue Right): Each CRR auction bid must name a TOU block. [e2]
- **Link to exchanges-ice-nodal** (cites e3, e4): used-in ICE & Nodal Exchange: ICE lists monthly ERCOT North futures for a peak block and an off-peak block. [e3][e4]
- **Link to hb-north** (cites e8, e3, e4): related HB_NORTH (North Hub): The ICE contracts reference the ERCOT North 345KV Hub real-time price. [e8][e3][e4]
- **Link to trading-hub** (cites e8): related Trading Hubs: The ICE reference price is the ERCOT North 345KV Hub real-time price published by ERCOT. [e8]
- **Link to shape-risk** (cites e1): related Shape Risk: A block covers only the hours defined for it, so hours outside the block are not covered by it. [e1]
- **Link to ptp-obligation** (cites e19, e1): related PTP Obligation: The Protocols evaluate a PTP Obligation hourly, and the Protocols require a CRR block to have the same MW amount in each hour. [e19][e1]
- **Link to settlement-point** (cites e35): related Settlement Point: A CRR block is bid between a source Settlement Point and a sink Settlement Point. [e35]
- **Link to heat-rate-call-option** (cites e26): related Heat Rate Call Option: ICE also lists an ERCOT heat rate contract with an off-peak schedule that mirrors the off-peak hours above. [e26]
- **Link to forward-curve** (cites e3, e4, e23): related Forward Curve: ICE lists monthly peak and off-peak futures that settle on ERCOT North peak and off-peak hours. [e3][e4][e23]
- **Link to qse** (cites e31): related QSE (Qualified Scheduling Entity): ERCOT's training material describes bilateral trades as QSE-to-QSE transactions that transfer settlement responsibility. [e31]
