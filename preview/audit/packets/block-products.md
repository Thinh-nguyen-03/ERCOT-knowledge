## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): In the ERCOT CRR auction, a block is a time-of-use (TOU) block having the same MW amount for each hour within the block. The Protocols auction CRRs in three one-month blocks: 5x16 (hours ending 0700-2200, Monday through Friday, excluding NERC holidays), 2x16 (hours ending 0700-2200, Saturday, Sunday and NERC holidays) and 7x8 (hours ending 0100-0600 and 2300-2400, every day). [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e1, e2, e6): The notation is days times hours: 5x16 is five weekdays of 16 hours, 2x16 is the weekend and holiday days of the same 16 hours, and 7x8 is the eight overnight hours on all seven days. The Protocols define the hour ranges directly. [e1] Every CRR auction bid or PCRR nomination must name one of these TOU blocks. [e2] In the CRR Monthly Auction only, one bid can cover all hours of the month as a linked offer across the three blocks, and because the three blocks together cover every hour, that linked bid spans all hours of the month. [e1][e6]
- **How it works #2** (cites e3, e5, e4): ICE uses the same split for its ERCOT North monthly futures. The peak future settles on the average of the peak hourly prices, with the specified price being the average of settlement point prices for hours ending 0700-2200 CPT. [e3][e5] The off-peak future averages hours ending 0100-0600 and 2300-2400 on weekdays and all hours on Saturdays, Sundays and NERC holidays. [e4]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e1, e5, e4, e7): The CRR auction rules and the ICE contracts line up on hours: the 0700-2200 window on weekdays (excluding NERC holidays) is the peak block, and the remaining hours form the off-peak block (the CRR rules split it into 2x16 and 7x8). [e1][e5][e4] The saved ICE specifications do not use the label "5x16" themselves. The peak future's pricing dates are Monday through Friday excluding NERC holidays. [e7]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - CRR TOU blocks are 5x16, 2x16 and 7x8, each in one-month strips. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e2): - CRR bids and PCRR nominations must specify a TOU block. [e2]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e8, e3, e9, e10): - ICE's ERCOT North peak and off-peak futures are monthly and cash settled, referencing the ERCOT North 345KV Hub real-time price. [e8][e3][e9][e10]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e5): - The peak average uses hours ending 0700-2200 CPT. [e5]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e6): - A single-month block bid is allowed only in the CRR Monthly Auction. [e6]
- **Say it like this #1** (cites e1, e5, e4): (Guidance) ERCOT's standard blocks split the week into the 16 hours ending 0700-2200 on weekdays (5x16), the same hours on weekends and holidays (2x16), and the eight hours ending 0100-0600 and 2300-2400 every day (7x8). [e1] Blocks of these are how CRRs are auctioned [e1], and ICE's peak future averages the 0700-2200 hours [e5] while its off-peak future averages the remaining hours. [e4]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e1, e4, e6): Off-peak is not one block in the CRR rules: it is the 2x16 weekend and holiday block plus the 7x8 overnight block, while ICE's off-peak future bundles those hours into one contract price. [e1][e4] Also 7x24 is not an auctioned block on its own; it is only a linked monthly bid. [e6]
- **Questions #2** (cites e1): Hours ending 0700-2200, Monday through Friday, excluding NERC holidays. [e1]
- **Questions #4** (cites e8, e3, e5): The average of the daily peak-hour prices published by ERCOT for the ERCOT North 345KV Hub real-time price, using hours ending 0700-2200 CPT. [e8][e3][e5]
- **Questions #6** (cites e6): Only through a single block bid in the CRR Monthly Auction, which links the three TOU blocks. [e6]
