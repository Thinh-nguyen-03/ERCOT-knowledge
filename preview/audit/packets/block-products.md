## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e11): In the ERCOT CRR auction, a block is a time-of-use (TOU) block having the same MW amount for each hour within the block. The Protocols auction CRRs in three one-month blocks: 5x16 (hours ending 0700-2200, Monday through Friday, excluding NERC holidays), 2x16 (hours ending 0700-2200, Saturday, Sunday and NERC holidays) and 7x8 (hours ending 0100-0600 and 2300-2400, every day). [e1] The Protocols also define "On-Peak Hours" as hours ending 0700 to 2200 CPT, Monday through Friday, excluding NERC holidays. [e11]
- **How it works #1** (cites e1, e30, e2, e19): The notation is days times hours: 5x16 is five weekdays of 16 hours, 2x16 is the weekend and holiday days of the same 16 hours, and 7x8 is the eight overnight hours on all seven days. The Protocols define the hour ranges directly. [e1] ERCOT's Forward Markets 101 training shows the same blocks as off-peak 0100-0600, peak weekday 0700-2200, peak weekend 0700-2200 and off-peak 2300-0000. [e30] Every CRR auction bid or PCRR nomination must name one of these TOU blocks. [e2] Because a PTP Obligation is evaluated hourly, the block's single MW amount applies to every hour in it. [e19][e1]
- **How it works #2** (cites e6, e20, e17, e21, e12, e13, e14, e15, e16): A 7x24 (around-the-clock) position is not a fourth auctioned block. In the CRR Monthly Auction only, one bid can cover all hours of the month as a linked offer across the three blocks. [e6] The bid criteria allow a 7x24 block in a CRR Monthly Auction but not in a CRR Auction held as part of a CRR Long-Term Auction Sequence (ERCOT's training slide carries the same rule), and the offer criteria say a 7x24 offer may not be designated. [e20][e17][e21] ERCOT's (2020 edition) training treats a 7x24 block bid as a single bid across all three TOU blocks and shows a worked example: with February's 672 hours split into 224 off-peak, 128 peak weekend and 320 peak weekday hours, and TOU clearing prices of $2, $3 and $8, the hour-weighted average is $5.048, so a $6 bid is awarded. [e12][e13][e14][e15][e16]
