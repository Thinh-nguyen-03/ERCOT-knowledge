## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to texas-legislature** (cites e1, e3): related Texas Legislature: The Legislature created CREZ through SB 408 in 2005. [e1][e3]
- **Link to puct** (cites e1, e4) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 25.174: related PUCT (Public Utility Commission of Texas): SB 408 directed the PUCT to facilitate the transmission process, and 16 TAC 25.174 sets how the PUCT designates zones and orders transmission. [e1][e4]
- **Link to wind-generation** (cites e3): related Wind Generation: The CREZ transmission was planned to connect roughly 18 GW of wind capacity. [e3]
- **Link to congestion** (cites e11): related Transmission Congestion: The PUCT rule lets the commission act if excess development in a CREZ causes congestion that SCED cannot resolve. [e11]
- **Link to gtc** (cites e19): related GTC (Generic Transmission Constraint): The Panhandle and West Texas Export GTCs are the constraints the IMM tied to Panhandle and West generation export; the IMM linked the fall of the Panhandle GTC's cost in 2022 to completed CREZ maintenance outages. [e19]
- **Link to binding-constraint** (cites e15): affects Binding Constraint: The IMM expected the physical limit of the West to North interface constraint to rise as CREZ transmission projects were completed. [e15]
- **Link to hb-west** (cites e16): related HB_WEST (West Hub): The IMM ties low West-zone prices to high wind output and congested interfaces out of the West. [e16]
- **Link to tdsp** (cites e7): related TDSP (Transmission & Distribution Service Provider): Under the PUCT rule the commission selects the entity or entities responsible for constructing CREZ transmission improvements. [e7]
- **Link to nodal-market** (cites e11): related Nodal Market: The CREZ rule refers to the security constrained economic dispatch mechanism used to establish dispatch priority of CREZ resources, and lets the commission consider dispatch priorities if it is insufficient. [e11]
