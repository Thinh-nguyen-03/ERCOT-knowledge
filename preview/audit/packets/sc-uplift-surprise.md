- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e3, e15, e31, e22, e7, e26, e27): Step 1, name the bucket. [e7][e26][e27]
- **How it works #2** (cites e5, e6, e25, e26, e27, e28, e13) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 25.520: Step 2, explain the allocation. Load Ratio Share is the ratio of an Entity's AML (retail Load usage data adjusted for UFE, losses and DC Tie exports) to total ERCOT AML for an interval; for a 15-minute interval a QSE's LRS is its AML (floored at zero) over total ERCOT AML. [e5][e6][e25] Several uplift items are spread on it: total RMR cost goes to QSEs representing Loads by LRS, ERCOT allocates the net RENA amount by LRS to stay revenue-neutral in each Settlement Interval, and Real-Time AS payments and charges are allocated to QSEs representing Load by LRS. [e26][e27][e28] The IMM, in its summary of the FFSS rule (16 TAC 25.520), also says ERCOT uplifts most FFSS program costs to LSEs by load-ratio share. [e13]
- **How it works #3** (cites e7, e10, e48, e23, e24): Step 3, explain RUC specifically. For RUC, QSEs that were capacity-short pay first, and only the rest of the make-whole cost is uplifted to all QSEs by Load Ratio Share; RUC clawback revenues are paid back to QSEs on the same basis, and RUC Decommitment Payments are charged back on LRS. [e7][e10][e48] A capacity-short QSE pays its shortfall ratio share of the total make-whole payments for that RUC, capped at two times the total make-whole payments times its shortfall over the total capacity of RUC-committed Resources. [e23][e24]
- **How it works #4** (cites e8, e11, e9, e21, e40, e41, e45) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 39%: Step 4, explain the cause. A make-whole payment reimburses allowable startup and minimum energy costs that a RUC-committed Resource did not recover in the market. [e8] RUC instructions rose to 5,129 resource-hours in 2025, 77% for congestion, and make-whole payments exceeded $21 million against $4.5 million of claw-backs. [e11][e9] The IMM attributes part of the rise to more RUC and fewer resources opting out of RUC settlement; opting out means giving up both make-whole and claw-back, and after NPRR1172 imposed a full claw-back on resources that opted out, the opt-out rate fell. [e21][e40][e41] The IMM's table shows the opt-out frequency at 3% in 2025, against 39% in 2021. [e45]

[e16][e17][e42] A positive amount on a statement is owed to ERCOT; a negative amount is owed by ERCOT. [e18]
