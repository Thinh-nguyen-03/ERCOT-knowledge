## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): This scenario asks an analyst to explain an unexpected uplift line on a settlement statement to a finance team. The IMM describes uplift as the non-market-based expenses loads pay on top of energy and ancillary services. [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e3, e15, e14, e22): Step 1, name the bucket. The IMM's uplift covers a list of settlements including Revenue Neutrality Allocation, emergency energy, ERS, black start, Firm Fuel, RMR, RUC, voltage services and the ERCOT administrative fee. [e2] In 2025 uplift totalled $668 million, or $1.38 per MWh of the all-in price, up 12% from 2024; RMR at $68 million was the largest contributor to the increase. [e3] Real-time congestion shortfalls are charged to LSEs through the Revenue Neutrality Allocation, and the IMM's 2025 annual table (in $ millions) lists Revenue Neutrality Uplift at $154. [e15][e14][e22]
- **How it works #2** (cites e5, e6, e7, e10, e13): Step 2, explain the allocation. Load Ratio Share is the ratio of an Entity's AML (retail Load usage data adjusted for UFE, losses and DC Tie exports) to total ERCOT AML for an interval, and several uplift items are spread on it. [e5][e6] For RUC, QSEs that were capacity-short pay first, and only the rest of the make-whole cost is uplifted to all QSEs by Load Ratio Share; RUC clawback revenues are paid back to QSEs on the same basis. [e7][e10] ERCOT also uplifts most FFSS costs to LSEs by load-ratio share. [e13]
- **How it works #3** (cites e8, e11, e9, e21): Step 3, explain the cause. A make-whole payment reimburses allowable startup and minimum energy costs that a RUC-committed Resource did not recover in the market. [e8] RUC instructions rose to 5,129 resource-hours in 2025, 77% for congestion, and make-whole payments exceeded $21 million against $4.5 million of claw-backs. [e11][e9] The IMM attributes part of the rise to more RUC and fewer resources opting out of RUC settlement. [e21]
- **How it works #4** (cites e16, e17, e18): Step 4, explain the timing and sign. ERCOT issues the RTM Initial Statement on the fifth day after the Operating Day and the RTM Final Statement on the 55th day, unless that day is not a Business Day. [e16][e17] A positive amount on a statement is owed to ERCOT; a negative amount is owed by ERCOT. [e18]
