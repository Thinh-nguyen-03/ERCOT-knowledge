- **Link to puct** (cites e9): related PUCT (Public Utility Commission of Texas): SB 2627 directed the Public Utility Commission of Texas to accept loan applications and approve or deny each one. [e9]
- **Link to texas-legislature** (cites e17): related Texas Legislature: SB 2627, the Powering Texas Forward Act, created the fund; the 89th Legislature then passed SB 2268 to adjust some caps, requirements and deadlines. [e17]
- **Link to resource-adequacy** (cites e15): related Resource Adequacy: ERCOT's CDR has scenarios that vary the amount and timing of TEF project capacity. [e15]
- **Link to cdr-report** (cites e11): used-in CDR Report: The CDR's Scenario 2 assumes all TEF projects are in service on their reported dates. [e11]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e3, e6): contrasts-with Energy Storage Resource (ESR): Electric energy storage facilities are not eligible for the loan or the completion bonus grant. [e3][e6]
- **Link to energy-only-market** (cites e10): related Energy-Only Market Design: Funded facilities must participate in the ERCOT wholesale electricity market. [e10]
- **Link to net-load** (cites e22): measures Net Load: Completion bonus grant performance is assessed over the 100 hours of highest peak net load, defined as gross load minus wind, solar and storage injection. [e22]
- **Link to hsl-lsl** (cites e24, e42): related HSL / LSL: Both the grant PRF and the loan PAF are ratios of a resource's real-time telemetered HSL to its obligated capacity. [e24][e42]
- **Link to mora-report** (cites e69): related MORA Report: ERCOT resource lists tag TEF projects by name, for example "(TEF)" after Cedar Bayou 5 and NRG THW GT 345. [e69]
- **Link to weatherization** (cites e75): related Weatherization Standards: A completion bonus grant application must include a narrative on preparations for compliance with the PUCT weather emergency preparedness rule. [e75]
