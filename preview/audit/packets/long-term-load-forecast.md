## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **How it works #1** (cites e3, e57, e4, e62, e5): The 2025 forecast stacks its parts in a "waterfall": base economic + EV + LFL + adjusted contracts + adjusted officer letters, minus PV. [e3] A Contract was a prospective load with a signed agreement and a financial commitment; an Officer Letter was a request without a signed agreement, whose viability a TSP officer attested in a letter to an ERCOT officer. [e57] ERCOT did not take TSP-reported large loads at face value. It delayed their ramp schedules by 180 days, cut data center additions to 49.8% and cut officer-letter additions to 55.4%. [e4] The 49.8% factor was applied to non-crypto data center additions only. [e62] Appendix A of the 2025 report lists a 2031 summer peak of 144,522 MW; the table does not say whether it is the ERCOT Adjusted or the TSP Provided case. [e5]
- **How it works #2** (cites e37, e39, e27, e28, e29, e6) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 75 MW: The PUCT's rule 16 TAC §25.370 (effective March 1, 2026, Project 58480) changed the inputs, with interim compliance-plan criteria for the 2026 cycle (below). [e37][e39] A "large load customer" is one with total expected non-coincident peak demand of 75 MW or more at a single site. [e27] ERCOT must not include such a customer's demand in a forecast used for transmission planning or resource adequacy unless the customer executed an interconnection agreement under §25.194 and provided the disclosures and financial commitments that rule requires. [e28] A DSP submitting load data must attach a notarized attestation, sworn by someone with binding authority, that each large load customer meets the §25.194 interconnection-agreement criteria. [e29] The IMM summarizes the change as including large loads by development milestones, replacing the old signed-agreement-or-officer-letter method. [e6]
- **How it works #3** (cites e30, e31, e32, e61) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2025: ERCOT must use the DSP data, but it may adjust it in two ways. It may correct errors or remove withdrawn or cancelled requests without PUCT approval, but only if the DSP agrees. [e30] Any other adjustment needs PUCT approval, which the PUCT may give only if the adjustment is supported by actual historical realization rates or other objective, credible, independent information; ERCOT must file and publish market notice at least 30 days before the open meeting, unless the PUCT chooses to take it up earlier. [e31] The rule also lets ERCOT use different forecasts for different study scopes and horizons, and requires an annual assessment comparing past forecasts to outcomes, identifying sources of error and recommending improvements. [e32] The IMM, for its part, said it would submit its detailed analysis of the load forecast through the stakeholder process rather than in its 2025 report. [e61]
- **How it works #4** (cites e33, e63, e34, e35, e36, e38, e39): If ERCOT could not implement protocols in time, §25.370(g) required a compliance plan for the 2026 Regional Transmission Plan: load data due by April 1, 2026, and each included large load must have an executed agreement meeting listed criteria. [e33] The criteria include disclosure of any separate request for electric service that could change, delay or withdraw the interconnection request, site control (a lease of at least five years from the date the load expects to reach contracted peak demand, or a deed), a load ramping schedule if applicable, and a financial commitment such as security of $100,000 per MW of contracted peak demand. [e33][e63][e34] The other financial-commitment options are security equal to the DSP and TSP's expected cost of equipment with a lead time of at least six months, or a CIAC payment equal to the directly attributable interconnection costs. [e35] Until any protocols or compliance plan were in place, ERCOT had to keep using its prior forecast practices. [e36] ERCOT filed its proposed compliance plan in Project 58480 on January 22, 2026, and the plan sets interim criteria until the permanent §25.370 criteria can be implemented. [e38][e39]
