- **Link to qse** (cites e3): related QSE (Qualified Scheduling Entity): Each LSE must designate a QSE to perform QSE functions for it. [e3]

- **Link to noe** (cites e1): related NOIE (Non-Opt-In Entity: Munis & Co-ops): NOIEs that serve Load are LSEs. [e1]
- **Link to esi-id** (cites e6): related ESI ID: An ELSE assigns an ESI ID for each wholesale point of delivery. [e6]
- **Link to retail-choice** (cites e1): related Retail Choice / Competitive Retail Market: LSEs include Competitive Retailers and NOIEs that serve Load. [e1]
- **Link to tdsp** (cites e7, e4) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 16.3: contrasts-with TDSP (Transmission & Distribution Service Provider): TSPs and DSPs register with ERCOT as a TSP or DSP, which is separate from the LSE registration in Section 16.3. [e7][e4]
- **Link to mass-transition** (cites e10): related Mass Transition: An LSE serving Load that fails to cure a QSE Affiliation Breach faces a Mass Transition of its ESI IDs. [e10]
- **Link to load-ratio-share** (cites e22, e23): related Load Ratio Share (LRS): ERCOT assigns Ancillary Service obligations to QSEs from their LSEs' Load Ratio Shares. [e22][e23]
