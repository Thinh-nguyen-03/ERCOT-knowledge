## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) A NOIE is a municipal utility or co-op that has not opened its territory to retail competition, so it serves its own load rather than competing against REPs. In ERCOT it still registers with ERCOT, registers as an LSE if it serves Load, and each LSE applicant designates a QSE. Its legacy generation gets special treatment in CRRs and it can have its own load zone.
