## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e2): A NOIE is an Electric Cooperative (EC) or Municipally Owned Utility (MOU) that does not offer Customer Choice. [e1] An MOU is a utility owned, operated and controlled by a nonprofit corporation whose directors are appointed by one or more municipalities, or a utility owned, operated or controlled by a municipality. [e2]
- **How it works #1** (cites e5, e12, e3): Customer choice applies to every Texas retail customer except customers of cooperatives and municipally owned utilities that have not opted for it. [e5] PURA also bars a person or retail electric utility from providing retail electric service in the certificated area of a co-op or muni that has not adopted customer choice, but this does not prohibit service in multiply certificated service areas to customers of any other retail electric utility. [e12] An MOU or EC that does offer Customer Choice and sells at retail is a Competitive Retailer. [e3]
- **How it works #2** (cites e6, e7): Each MOU and EC must register with ERCOT and sign the agreements for the functions it performs, whether it plans to be a NOIE or a Competitive Retailer. [e6] If it registers an affiliate as a CR, it must tell ERCOT six months before opting in. [e7]
- **How it works #3** (cites e11, e4, e15): Each MOU and EC assigns an ESI ID to each NOIE wholesale point of delivery, and those ESI IDs must be assigned to an LSE. [e11] NOIEs that serve Load are LSEs, so they register with ERCOT as LSEs [e4], and each LSE applicant must designate a QSE. [e15]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e8, e13, e9, e10): The Protocols carry NOIE-specific features. A NOIE or group of NOIEs can establish a NOIE Load Zone through a one-time election. [e8] A QSE offering services in a NOIE service territory must get written authorization from the NOIE and submit it to ERCOT. [e13] There is a CRR type, PTP Obligation with Links to an Option, defined around a NOIE's PTP Obligation and PTP Option. [e9] The IMM notes that ERCOT allocates Pre-Assigned CRRs to NOIEs based on generation ownership or arrangements that predate retail competition. [e10]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - NOIE = EC or MOU that does not offer Customer Choice. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e5): - Customer choice since January 1, 2002 excludes customers of co-ops and munis that have not opted in. [e5]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e7): - An MOU or EC registering an affiliate as a CR must notify ERCOT six months before opting in. [e7]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e11): - NOIE wholesale delivery points get ESI IDs assigned to an LSE. [e11]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e10) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2025: - PCRRs are allocated to NOIEs (IMM description, 2025 report). [e10]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e6, e1, e3, e16): (Guidance) "NOIE" and "opt-in" are not about whether the entity is in ERCOT; both opt-in and non-opt-in munis and co-ops can be inside the ERCOT Region and register with ERCOT. [e6] The difference is Customer Choice. [e1][e3] Also, the PUCT TDU definition excludes MOUs and ECs, so do not call a muni a TDU in PUCT terms. [e16]
- **Questions #2** (cites e1, e3): A NOIE is an EC or MOU that does not offer Customer Choice; a Competitive Retailer is a REP or an MOU or EC that does offer Customer Choice and sells at retail. [e1][e3]
- **Questions #4** (cites e7): The Protocols say that an MOU or EC that decides to register an affiliate as a CR must notify ERCOT six months before opting in. [e7] The saved sources do not lay out the full process.
- **Questions #6** (cites e10, e9): The IMM says NOIEs receive PCRRs allocated based on generation ownership or arrangements that predate retail competition, at a fraction of the auction value of comparable CRRs. [e10] The Protocols also define a CRR type linking a NOIE's PTP Obligation to its PTP Option. [e9]
- **Link to lse** (cites e4): part-of LSE (Load Serving Entity): NOIEs that serve Load are LSEs. [e4]
- **Link to retail-choice** (cites e1, e5): contrasts-with Retail Choice / Competitive Retail Market: NOIEs are the MOUs and ECs that do not offer Customer Choice. [e1][e5]
- **Link to tdsp** (cites e14): related TDSP (Transmission & Distribution Service Provider): MOUs and ECs operating as a TSP or DSP in the ERCOT Region must register as a TSP or DSP. [e14]
- **Link to crr** (cites e9, e10): related CRR (Congestion Revenue Right): NOIEs have special CRR products and allocations. [e9][e10]
- **Link to pcrr** (cites e10): related PCRR (Pre-Assigned CRR): The IMM says ERCOT allocates PCRRs to NOIEs, and that parties receiving PCRRs pay only a fraction of the auction value of comparable CRRs. [e10]
- **Link to load-zone** (cites e8): related Load Zone: Protocols let a NOIE or group of NOIEs establish a NOIE Load Zone. [e8]
## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) A NOIE is a municipal utility or co-op that has not opened its territory to retail competition, so it serves its own load rather than competing against REPs. In ERCOT it still registers with ERCOT, registers as an LSE if it serves Load, and each LSE applicant designates a QSE.
