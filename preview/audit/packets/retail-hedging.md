## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) A retailer's hedge is whatever covers its load against real-time prices: owned generation, bilateral PPAs, futures, or day-ahead positions. Day-ahead cover smooths volatility, but it does not shield against a sustained price rise. Exposure shares in the IMM report are measured from ERCOT settlement data, so they leave out bilateral hedges the IMM has no information on.
