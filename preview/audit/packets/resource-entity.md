## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1): A Resource Entity is an Entity that owns or controls a Generation Resource, an Energy Storage Resource, a Settlement Only Generator or a Load Resource and is registered with ERCOT as a Resource Entity. [e1]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e3, e5): Every Resource Entity operating in the ERCOT Region must register with ERCOT. [e2] To register it must execute the Standard Form Market Participant Agreement, name its Authorized Representatives, contacts and a User Security Administrator, and show ERCOT it can perform the functions of a Resource Entity under the Protocols. [e3] Before commissioning, Resource Entities regularly update the data ERCOT needs for modeling. [e5]
- **How it works #2** (cites e8, e9, e4, e6): A Resource Entity is the owner/controller role. A QSE is qualified to communicate with ERCOT for Resource Entities and to settle payments and charges with ERCOT. [e8] If ERCOT gives notice of a Resource Entity's QSE's termination or suspension, the Resource Entity is designated a Virtual QSE for up to two Bank Business Days, during which it must designate a new QSE or satisfy QSE creditworthiness requirements and operate as an Emergency QSE. [e9][e4] If an Emergency QSE that is a Resource Entity cannot meet the requirements in time, ERCOT may give it more time. [e6]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e7, e1): The word "Resource" used alone in the Protocols covers ESRs, Generation Resources and Load Resources, but not Settlement Only Generators or ERS Resources, even though a Resource Entity can own a SOG. [e7][e1] So the "Resource Entity" label is wider than the "Resource" label.
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - Owns or controls a Generation Resource, ESR, SOG or Load Resource. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e2, e3): - Must register with ERCOT, with a Standard Form Market Participant Agreement. [e2][e3]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e9): - Is designated a Virtual QSE for up to two Bank Business Days if ERCOT gives notice of its QSE's termination or suspension. [e9]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e6): - ERCOT may give an Emergency QSE that is a Resource Entity extra time to fix its QSE arrangements. [e6]
- **Say it like this #1** (cites e8): (Guidance) A Resource Entity is the registered owner or controller of the plants, batteries or controllable loads. It registers with ERCOT and relies on a QSE to communicate with ERCOT and settle charges. [e8]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e1, e7, e4, e9): (Guidance) A Resource Entity is the Entity that owns or controls Resources; "Resource" on its own means an ESR, Generation Resource or Load Resource. [e1][e7] A Resource Entity is also not a QSE, although the Virtual and Emergency QSE rules can make it act as one. [e4][e9]
- **Questions #2** (cites e9, e4, e6): The Resource Entity is designated a Virtual QSE for up to two Bank Business Days, during which it must designate a new QSE or satisfy QSE creditworthiness requirements and operate as an Emergency QSE. [e9][e4] If it ends up as an Emergency QSE and cannot meet the requirements in time, ERCOT may allow additional time. [e6]
- **Questions #4** (cites e7, e1): The Protocols use "Resource" alone to mean ESR, Generation Resource or Load Resource and exclude SOGs and ERS Resources, but a Resource Entity definition names SOGs among what it owns or controls. [e7][e1]
- **Link to qse** (cites e4): related QSE (Qualified Scheduling Entity): A Resource Entity can be designated a Virtual QSE by ERCOT to temporarily perform QSE responsibilities. [e4]
- **Link to resource-types** (cites e1): composed-of Resource Types (Generation, Load, ESR, SOG): The Resource types a Resource Entity may own or control are Generation Resources, ESRs, Load Resources and SOGs. [e1]
- **Link to lse** (cites e4): contrasts-with LSE (Load Serving Entity): LSEs and Resource Entities can both be designated Virtual QSEs by ERCOT. [e4]
- **Link to clr** (cites e1): related CLR (Controllable Load Resource): A Load Resource is among the Resources a Resource Entity owns or controls. [e1]
- **Link to dgr-sodg** (cites e1): related DGR & SODG (Distribution-Connected Generation): A Settlement Only Generator is among the things a Resource Entity owns or controls. [e1]
- **Link to energy-storage-resource** (cites e1): related Energy Storage Resource (ESR): An ESR is one of the Resource types a Resource Entity can own or control. [e1]
