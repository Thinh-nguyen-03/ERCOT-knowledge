- **Link to winter-storm-uri** (cites e24): part-of Winter Storm Uri (Feb 2021): The defaults came from charges incurred during the Winter Storm Uri period of emergency, defined in HB 4492 as February 12-20, 2021. [e24]
- **Link to securitization** (cites e6): causes Post-Uri Securitization: HB 4492 created a default balance of up to $800 million to finance amounts owed to ERCOT from the period of emergency. [e6]
- **Link to mass-transition** (cites e3, e12, e13): example-of Mass Transition: Customers of Griddy, Entrust and Power of Texas were moved to other retailers through ERCOT's mass transition process; ERCOT began Griddy's on February 26, 2021. [e3][e12][e13]
- **Link to uri-repricing** (cites e8) FLAG number-not-in-quote: $9,000: related Uri $9,000 Pricing Controversy: Brazos sought to cut more than $1.1 billion from ERCOT's claim, the amount tied to the PUCT's February 15 and 16 pricing orders. [e8]
- **Link to credit-tpe** (cites e46): related Credit, Collateral & TPE: The PUCT found Griddy violated PURA by not observing ERCOT's rules on collateral calls and invoice payment. [e46]
- **Link to rep** (cites e4): related REP (Retail Electric Provider): Several retail electric providers left the market after the storm. [e4]
- **Link to polr** (cites e55, e56): related POLR (Provider of Last Resort): In a mass transition ERCOT first assigns ESI IDs to volunteer REPs and then to large service providers by MWh market share. [e55][e56]
- **Link to uplift** (cites e18, e20) FLAG number-not-in-quote: $2.5: related Uplift: Protocol default uplift spreads unpaid amounts over QSEs and CRR Account Holders but charges no more than $2.5 million per set of invoices at least 30 days apart. [e18][e20]
- **Link to retail-hedging** (cites e3): related Retail Load Hedging: The UT report says retailers' losses depended on how well their price risk was hedged. [e3]
- **Link to settlement-statements** (cites e66, e22): related Settlement Statements & Timeline: Under Protocol 9.19, when a short-pay cannot be covered, ERCOT reduces payments pro rata to all invoice recipients owed money and then, if funds remain unavailable, uplifts the shortfall. [e66][e22]
