## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **How it works #1** (cites e3, e2, e4, e5, e12, e6): In the Protocols uplift shows up as specific mechanisms with different allocators. Load Ratio Share, the ratio of an Entity's AML to total ERCOT AML, is the allocator for the RUC make-whole shortfall. [e3][e2] The other main families are the revenue neutrality allocations, the default uplift for short-paid invoices, and the Securitization Uplift Charge for the Winter Storm Uri balance. [e4][e5][e12] A Cost Allocation Zone may also be used to uplift certain costs to a QSE's Load regardless of NOIE Load Zone. [e6]
- **How it works #2** (cites e14, e2, e13): RUC make-whole. QSEs that were capacity-short in a RUC are charged first: the charge is the QSE's shortfall ratio share times total RUC make-whole payments for that RUC, including RMR Units, subject to a cap. [e14] Only if those revenues do not cover the payments for a 15-minute interval is the difference uplifted to all QSEs by LRS. [e2][e13]
- **How it works #3** (cites e15, e17, e40, e18): Revenue neutrality (RENA). ERCOT must be revenue-neutral in each Settlement Interval, and each QSE receives an allocated share, on an LRS basis, of the net of a list of Real-Time energy payments and charges that includes Real-Time congestion payments or charges for Self-Schedules and Real-Time payments or charges to CRR Owners. [e15][e17] The IMM describes RENA as arising when day-ahead and real-time power flow modeling differ, for example through transmission model differences, differences between the LDF used in the DAM and actual real-time load distribution (the "LDF contribution"), and CRR uplift. [e40] A separate allocation spreads Real-Time Ancillary Service costs to QSEs representing Load by LRS. [e18]
- **How it works #4** (cites e47, e48, e20, e19, e4, e23): Default uplift. If a short-paying Entity does not pay, ERCOT draws on available Financial Security, offsets or recoups amounts it owes that Entity, and, if it is still short, deducts certain fees and reduces payments to all owed parties pro rata; where funds remain unavailable and the Entity is not following a payment plan, it uplifts the short-paid amount through the Default Uplift process. [e47][e48][e20][e19] ERCOT collects the month's short-pay amount, less expected payment-plan payments, from QSEs and CRR Account Holders and pays it to the Entities that were short-paid. [e4][e23]
- **How it works #5** (cites e26, e29): Securitization Uplift Charge. ERCOT allocates the charge for each Operating Day to QSEs representing obligated LSEs. [e26] See the Formula section for the allocator. The charge may not be passed through to transmission-voltage Customers that are Securitization Uplift Charge Opt-Out Entities. [e29]
- **How it works #6** (cites e7, e11) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2024: The IMM's own grouping is wider. In its 2024 report it counts the reliability and ORDC adder imbalance settlements, Revenue Neutrality Allocation, emergency energy charges, base point deviation payments, ERS, black start, block load transfer, firm fuel, high dispatch limit override, RMR, RUC, voltage services and the ERCOT administrative fee as uplift for its all-in price. [e7] The ORDC adder in that list was discontinued in December 2025 with the introduction of RTC. [e11]
- **Formula #1** (cites e13, e2): RUC make-whole uplift charge (np-05 5.7.4.2): the QSE's charge is minus one times (total RUC make-whole payments for the hour, divided by 4 for a 15-minute interval, plus the total RUC capacity-short charges for the interval) times the QSE's Load Ratio Share. [e13] The capacity-short charge total is netted into that amount, so what is uplifted is the difference left after those revenues. [e13][e2]
- **Formula #2** (cites e16) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 6.6: RENA (np-06 6.6.10): each QSE's Load-Allocated Real-Time Revenue Neutrality Amount is minus one times the sum of the total net real-time amounts (energy imbalance, Block Load Transfer, DC import, SODG/SOTG energy, real-time congestion cost, and the real-time obligation totals divided by 4) multiplied by the QSE's LRS. [e16] A pending revision (NPRR995) would change one of the terms in that list; this entry cites the current text. [e16]
- **Formula #3** (cites e49, e21, e25, e24): Default uplift (np-09 9.19.1): each Counter-Party's share, DURSCP, equals the total short-pay amount to be uplifted (TSPA) times the Counter-Party's Maximum MWh Activity Ratio Share (MMARS), which is its Maximum MWh Activity (MMA) divided by the total of all Counter-Parties' MMA. MMA is the largest of several MWh activity measures for the Counter-Party in the reference month, which is the month before the default; the first two measures in the Max { ... } list are a sum of the URTMG, URTDCIMP and USOGTOT terms and a sum of the URTAML and UWSLTOT terms, followed by further terms. [e49][e21] ERCOT posts the MMA, MMATOT, MMARS and the Counter-Party level components monthly on the MIS Certified Area. [e25] The formula has a pending replacement for applicable portions of MMA (NPRR995 and NPRR1201), which is not the rule in force. [e24] (The full variable table for MMA did not extract cleanly; the measure list here is drawn from the formula text and is partial.)
- **Formula #4** (cites e26, e27, e28) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 27.3: Securitization Uplift Charge (np-27 27.3): the charge to QSE q for Operating Day d equals the Securitization Uplift Charge Daily Amount times the QSE's Daily QSE Non-Opted-Out LSE Load Ratio Share, which is the QSE's non-opted-out LSE RTAML divided by the ERCOT total for the day. [e26][e27] ERCOT re-evaluates at least quarterly so the charge is repaid in substantially equal payments over its term. [e28]
