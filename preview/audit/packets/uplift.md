## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e2): "Uplift" is not a defined term in the Protocol definitions (np-02). The Protocols use it in several named charges, and the IMM uses it as a label for non-market-based costs that loads pay on top of energy and ancillary services. [e1] In the Protocols the idea is that a cost is "uplifted" to Market Participants, for example the RUC make-whole shortfall to all QSEs on a Load Ratio Share basis. [e2]
- **How it works #1** (cites e2, e4, e5, e12, e3, e6): In the Protocols uplift shows up as specific mechanisms: the RUC Make-Whole Uplift Charge, default uplift invoices that collect short-paid amounts from QSEs and CRR Account Holders, and the Securitization Uplift Charge for the Winter Storm Uri securitization balance. [e2][e4][e5][e12] Load Ratio Share, the ratio of an Entity's AML to total ERCOT AML, is the allocator for the RUC make-whole shortfall. [e3][e2] A Cost Allocation Zone may be used to uplift certain costs to a QSE's Load regardless of NOIE Load Zone. [e6]
- **How it works #2** (cites e7, e11) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2024: In its 2024 report the IMM groups many settlements under uplift for its all-in price: the reliability and ORDC adder imbalance settlements, Revenue Neutrality Allocation, emergency energy charges, base point deviation payments, ERS, black start, block load transfer, firm fuel, high dispatch limit override, RMR, RUC, voltage services and the ERCOT administrative fee. [e7] The ORDC adder in that list was discontinued in December 2025 with the introduction of RTC. [e11]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e10, e8, e9): The IMM put uplift at $1.24 per MWh in 2024, about $573 million in total, and $1.38 per MWh in 2025, with $668 million in total and RMR the largest contributor to the increase. [e10][e8] The IMM also notes that real-time congestion shortfalls are charged to LSEs through RENA. [e9]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e2, e4, e5): - No np-02 definition of "uplift"; usage is by mechanism. [e2][e4][e5]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e2): - RUC make-whole shortfall is uplifted to all QSEs by LRS. [e2]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e10): - 2024 uplift: about $573 million, $1.24 per MWh. [e10]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e8): - 2025 uplift: $668 million, $1.38 per MWh, up 12%. [e8]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e9): - RENA is an uplift charge to LSEs. [e9]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e2, e4, e5, e7): Not every uplift item is allocated the same way: RUC make-whole uses Load Ratio Share, default uplift is collected from QSEs and CRR Account Holders, and the Securitization Uplift Charge is tied to obligated LSEs. [e2][e4][e5] Also, the IMM's uplift list is its analytical grouping for the all-in price. [e7]
- **Questions #2** (cites e2, e5): No definition was found in the definitions section; the Protocols name specific uplift charges. [e2][e5]
- **Questions #4** (cites e10, e8): Approximately $573 million in 2024 and $668 million in 2025, per the IMM. [e10][e8]
- **Questions #6** (cites e9): Real-time congestion shortfalls are paid through RENA charges to LSEs. [e9]
- **Link to ruc-make-whole** (cites e2): related RUC Make-Whole & Clawback: RUC make-whole costs not covered by capacity-short charges are uplifted to all QSEs by Load Ratio Share. [e2]
- **Link to load-ratio-share** (cites e3): used-in Load Ratio Share (LRS): Load Ratio Share is the ratio of an Entity's AML to total ERCOT AML for an interval. [e3]
- **Link to settlement-statements** (cites e2): related Settlement Statements & Timeline: Some uplifts, such as the RUC make-whole shortfall, are allocated to all QSEs. [e2]
- **Link to securitization** (cites e5, e12): related Post-Uri Securitization: The Securitization Uplift Charge is used to pay the Securitization Uplift Balance, which the Protocols define as the amount ERCOT securitized under PURA Subchapter N, Winter Storm Uri Uplift Financing. [e5][e12]
- **Link to crr** (cites e9): related CRR (Congestion Revenue Right): The IMM says real-time congestion shortfall costs are paid through the uplift charge known as RENA. [e9]
## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) Uplift is the part of your all-in cost that is not energy or ancillary services: settlement items spread over load rather than priced into the market. It is the IMM's grouping for the all-in price; the Protocols name specific uplift charges but do not define "uplift" itself.
