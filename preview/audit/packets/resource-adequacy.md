## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ERCOT now has a formal reliability target of one loss-of-load event in ten years and must check every three years whether the market is delivering it. The planning reports show reserve margins falling significantly year to year, while real-time reserves have been ample. That gap is the core of the current resource adequacy debate.
