## Statements to check (new or changed since the last audit)

- **Link to rep** (cites e6, e7): contrasts-with REP (Retail Electric Provider): A TDU may not sell electricity, while a REP sells at retail but may not own generation. [e6][e7]
- **Link to lse** (cites e4): contrasts-with LSE (Load Serving Entity): TSPs and DSPs register with ERCOT as a TSP or DSP, under a registration section separate from the LSE registration. [e4]
- **Link to noe** (cites e4, e5): related NOIE (Non-Opt-In Entity: Munis & Co-ops): MOUs and ECs are included in the registration requirement for TSPs and DSPs, though the PUCT TDU definition excludes them. [e4][e5]
- **Link to esi-id** (cites e3): related ESI ID: A DSP owns or operates the Distribution System that delivers energy to Customers. [e3]
- **Link to ams-meters** (cites e8, e31) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 25.130: related AMS Smart Meter Data: PURA assigns metering to the TDU in the default case, and PUCT rule 25.130 applies to all electric utilities including TDUs. [e8][e31]
- **Link to retail-choice** (cites e9): part-of Retail Choice / Competitive Retail Market: From January 1, 2002 a TDU must provide transmission or distribution service to REPs and others on comparable terms. [e9]
- **Link to four-cp** (cites e15): related 4CP Transmission Charges: A TSP's transmission rate is its cost of service divided by the average of ERCOT coincident peak demand for June to September (4CP), and each DSP is charged on its 4CP demand. [e15]
- **Link to large-load-interconnection** (cites e42): related Large Load Interconnection & Batch Zero: PURA requires the PUCT's SB 6 large load standards to set a flat study fee of at least $100,000 payable to the interconnecting utility for initial transmission screening studies. [e42]
- **Link to mass-transition** (cites e13): related Mass Transition: A Mass Transition includes moving ESI IDs from one TDSP to another. [e13]
- **Link to load-shed** (cites e44): related Load Shed / Rotating Outages: PURA directs the PUCT to require TDUs (and cooperatives and municipally owned utilities) providing transmission service in ERCOT to submit critical load designations and a load shedding plan. [e44]
- **Link to sb6** (cites e51) FLAG number-not-in-quote: 2025: related SB 6 (2025) Large Load Legislation: SB 6 directs the PUCT to evaluate the 4CP method used to charge transmission costs to distribution providers. [e51]

## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) The TDSP owns and runs the poles and wires. It delivers power for any retailer on comparable terms and does not sell the power itself. Its transmission bill to the grid is set by its share of the four summer coincident peaks, and it passes changes through to retailers' customers in a TCRF.
