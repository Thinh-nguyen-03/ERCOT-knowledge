## Statements to check

- **Definition #1** (cites e1, e2, e3): A TDSP is an Entity that is a TSP, a DSP or both, or an Entity selected to own and operate Transmission Facilities that has a PUCT-approved code of conduct. [e1] A TSP owns or operates Transmission Facilities and provides Transmission Service in the ERCOT Transmission Grid, and a DSP owns or operates a Distribution System that delivers energy from the ERCOT Transmission Grid to Customers. [e2][e3]
- **How it works #1** (cites e4): Each TSP and DSP in the ERCOT Region, including MOUs and ECs, must register with ERCOT. Registration means complying with Operating Guide backup plan requirements, signing a Standard Form Market Participant Agreement, designating authorized representatives, contacts and a User Security Administrator, and being capable of performing TSP or DSP functions. [e4]
- **How it works #2** (cites e10): DSPs in areas where Customer Choice is in effect, including opt-in MOUs and opt-in co-ops, must complete Retail Market Testing before starting operations with ERCOT. [e10]
- **How it works #3** (cites e9): Under PURA, a transmission and distribution utility must provide transmission or distribution service at retail to REPs, utilities and end-use customers on rates, terms and conditions comparable to those applying to the utility and its affiliates. [e9]
- **ERCOT context #1** (cites e5, e4): The PUCT definition of "TDU" excludes MOUs and ECs, and says a TDU may be one utility or separate transmission and distribution utilities. [e5] The ERCOT term TDSP is wider because ERCOT's registration requirement reaches MOUs and ECs that operate transmission or distribution. [e4]
- **ERCOT context #2** (cites e6, e8): Since January 1, 2002 a TDU may not sell electricity or otherwise participate in the electricity market except to buy electricity for its own needs. [e6] In customer-choice areas, metering stays with the TDU affiliate of the electric utility that served the area before choice began, with a competitive exception for interval-data-recorder customers that PURA describes. [e8]
- **Key facts #1** (cites e1): - TDSP = TSP, DSP, or both. [e1]
- **Key facts #2** (cites e4): - TSPs and DSPs, including MOUs and ECs in the ERCOT Region, must register with ERCOT. [e4]
- **Key facts #3** (cites e6): - A TDU may not sell electricity after January 1, 2002 (other than buying for its own needs). [e6]
- **Key facts #4** (cites e9): - TDUs must provide transmission or distribution service to REPs and others on comparable terms. [e9]
- **Key facts #5** (cites e10): - DSPs in customer-choice areas must complete Retail Market Testing. [e10]
- **Common confusion #1** (cites e4, e5, e6): (Guidance) TDU (a PUCT/PURA term) and TDSP (an ERCOT Protocol term) overlap but are not identical: the PUCT TDU definition excludes MOUs and ECs, while ERCOT registration of TSPs and DSPs includes them. [e4][e5] A TDU may not sell electricity, so it is not a retailer. [e6]
- **Questions #2** (cites e6, e7): PURA bars a transmission and distribution utility from selling electricity or otherwise participating in the electricity market, other than buying electricity to serve its own needs, after January 1, 2002. [e6] Retail sales belong to REPs and other competitive retailers. [e7]
- **Questions #4** (cites e2, e3): A TSP owns or operates Transmission Facilities and provides Transmission Service; a DSP owns or operates the Distribution System that delivers energy from the transmission grid to Customers. [e2][e3]
- **Link to rep** (cites e6, e7): contrasts-with REP (Retail Electric Provider): A TDU may not sell electricity, while a REP sells at retail but may not own generation. [e6][e7]
- **Link to lse** (cites e4): contrasts-with LSE (Load Serving Entity): TSPs and DSPs register with ERCOT as a TSP or DSP, under a registration section separate from the LSE registration. [e4]
- **Link to noe** (cites e4, e5): related NOIE (Non-Opt-In Entity: Munis & Co-ops): MOUs and ECs are included in the registration requirement for TSPs and DSPs, though the PUCT TDU definition excludes them. [e4][e5]
- **Link to esi-id** (cites e3): related ESI ID: A DSP owns or operates the Distribution System that delivers energy to Customers. [e3]
- **Link to ams-meters** (cites e8): related AMS Smart Meter Data: PURA assigns metering to the TDU in the default case. [e8]
- **Link to retail-choice** (cites e9): part-of Retail Choice / Competitive Retail Market: From January 1, 2002 a TDU must provide transmission or distribution service to REPs and others on comparable terms. [e9]
## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) The TDSP owns and runs the poles and wires. It delivers power for any retailer on comparable terms and does not sell the power itself.
