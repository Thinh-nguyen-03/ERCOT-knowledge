## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) An NPRR is the formal route for changing the ERCOT rulebook. It runs through the PRS and TAC committees and the ERCOT Board, and nothing takes effect until the PUCT approves it.
