[e35][e36] The Segments are Cooperative, Independent Generator, Independent Power Marketer, Independent REP, Investor-Owned Utility, Municipal and Consumer (with Commercial, Industrial and Residential subsegments). [e38] The Board may take requests that TAC unanimously recommended on a consent agenda, and any Director can pull an item off it. [e39]
