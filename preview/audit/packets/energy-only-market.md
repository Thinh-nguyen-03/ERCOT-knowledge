## Uncited guidance text (check for new facts, numbers, dates, names)

- (Say it like this) (Guidance) ERCOT does not pay for capacity. Generators earn their fixed costs back mostly in a few tight hours when shortage pricing kicks in, so investment depends on expected scarcity, not a guaranteed payment. That makes revenue lumpy: one soft year does not mean the design has failed, but it does fuel the debate over whether price signals alone can deliver the reliability standard.
