Dear Chairman and Commissioners:
Attached for your consideration is a brief addendum to the Permian Basin Reliability Plan
Study ERCOT filed on July 25,2024. This addendum identifies a new endpoint for 345-kV Import
Path 4, which is one of the 345-kV import path options identified in ERCOT' s original study. As
explained in the addendum, the new endpoint recommended for Import Path 4 better aligns with the
load and generation patterns identified in ERCOT's initial 2024 Regional Transmission Plan (RTP)

results.
One important incidental benefit to this new endpoint is that it would better align with the
"alternative stage two" recommendation identified by Commission Staff' s memorandum filed on
September 7, 2024. Under that alternative recommendation, the Commission would authorize
Transmission Service Providers (TSP) to begin certain preparatory work for both 345-kV and 765kV import path options while a final decision on voltage level is pending. The updated endpoint for
Import Path 4, as described in the addendum, would provide for additional commonality in the rights

of way needed for the 345-kV and 765-kV options should the Commission choose to accept Staff' s
alternative recommendation. For the Commission' s benefit, I note that ERCOT has no concern with
Commission Staff' s primary recommendation or its alternative stage two recommendation.
Thank you for your consideration of this addendum. ERCOT would be pleased to provide
any additional information you may need in evaluating the Permian Basin Reliability Plan.
Respectfully submitted,

Addendum
On July 25,2024, ERCOT submitted the Reliability Plan forthe Permian Basin Region as directed by
the Public Utility Commission of Texas (Commission) in its December 14, 2023 order. The Reliability
Plan identifies the transmission facilities needed to meet the forecasted demand in the Permian Basin
region of Texas.
ERCOT is submitting this addendum to the Reliability Plan for the Permian Basin Region to change
the recommended endpoint of one of the 345-kV import paths, Import Path 4. Specifically, ERCOT
now recommends that this Import Path 4 should terminate at Howard 345-kV instead of Fowlerton
345-kV. This change will require the addition of a new 345-kV substation-"New Substation 3"-in
place of the proposed new Hamilton 345-kV substation. ERCOT is recommending this change to the
endpoint of Import Path 4 to better accommodate the changing load and generation patterns across
the larger system, as identified in the initial 2024 Regional Transmission Plan (RTP). ERCOT has
conducted additional reliability assessments consistent with the methodology identified in the Permian
Basin Reliability Plan Study and confirmed that the proposed new endpoint to Import Path 4 meets the
overall reliability need and performance of the 345-kV import path in the Permian Basin Reliability Plan
Study. Figure 1.1 shows the original proposed 345-kV import paths for 2038, and Figure 1.2 shows
the modified 345-kV import paths for 2038.
Modified Import Path 4:
·

Construct a new 345-kV "New Substation 3" substation. Add new dynamic reactive devices
(350 MVAr) at New Substation 3 345-kV substation; and

·

Add new Howard - New Substation 3 - Bottlebrush - Solstice 345-kV double-circuit lines with
a normal and emergency rating of at Ieast 2988 MVA percircuit. This will require approximately
370 miles of new right of way (ROW).
