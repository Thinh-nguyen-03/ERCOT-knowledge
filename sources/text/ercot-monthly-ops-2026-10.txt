ERCOT identified one pricing incident, but it did not meet the criteria to
correct prices

Did Not Meet Criteria
On Operating Day October 08, 2026, ERCOT performed a scheduled site failover and activated
Emergency Basepoints. ERCOT’s analysis showed an estimated absolute total dollar impact of
$281.53 for changes to Real-Time prices for energy metered. This impact did not meet the
significance criteria of $500 and so ERCOT did not correct prices.
