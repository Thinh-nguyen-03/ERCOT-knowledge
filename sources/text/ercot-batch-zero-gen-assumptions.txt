Batch Zero Interconnection Study Generation Need

• As presented at the September Board meeting, the Batch Zero conditional base and
studied load total ranges from around 100 GW in 2028 to around 192 GW in 2032
• The total load forecast may potentially reach 200 GW for 2028 and 300 GW for 2032
• The total available generation in the interconnection queue will not be sufficient to meet
the anticipated load in the Batch Zero Interconnection Study under certain system
conditions (e.g., no solar)
• As outlined in PGRR127, additional generation outside of the interconnection queue may
need to be added

Potential Options for Generation Assumptions

• ERCOT is considering the following to address the generation need in the Batch Zero
Interconnection Study
o Incorporate generation with a later in-service-date into earlier study years
o Address the generation need under certain system conditions using one or a combination of
the following options
✓ Dispatch batteries to a higher level than the previous planning studies; or
✓ Dispatch both batteries and wind to a higher level than the previous planning studies; or
✓ Add additional generators that are not in the current interconnection queue
