Shortage (ORDC) and Reliability Adder Floor Impact on Energy Cost

W ith implementation of RTC, there is no longer an explicit shortage price adder
calculated or produced by the market software. The energy price natively
includes any shortage value produced by the ASDC via co-optimization. As such,
there is no information available to produce this chart going forward.
