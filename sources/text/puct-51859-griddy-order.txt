ORDER
This Order addresses Commission Staff's petition to revoke the retail electric provider
(REP) certificate of Griddy Energy LLC.

Commission Staff filed an agreement between

Commission Staff, Griddy, the Office of Public Utility Counsel (OPUC), and the Electric
Reliability Council of Texas, Inc. (ERCOT) to this proceeding. Intervenors AEP Texas Inc..
CenterPoint Energy Houston Electric, LLC; and Texas-New Mexico Power Company (TNMP)

are unopposed to the agreement. For the reasons identified in this Order, the Commission
revokes Griddy's REP certificate number 10243.

Financial Obligation to ERCOT

27.

Vrom February 16,2021 through February 24,2021, ERCOT issued a series of collateral

calls and settlement invoices requiring Griddy to provide collateral in a cumulative amount

approximately $30.6 million.
28.

February 22,2021. Griddy did not provide ERCOT with the required collateral to cure the
payment breach.

30.

February 25,2021.

II.

Conclusions of Law

The Commission makes the following conclusions of law.

1.

The Commission has jurisdiction over this matter under PURA §§ 14.051.39.1510).

and 39.356(a).
2.

Before this Order , Griddy was a retail electric provider in ERCOT as the term retail electric
provider is defined in 16 Texas Administrative Code (TAC) § 25.5(114).

3.

Commission Staff provided notice of the petition in accordance with the requirements of
Texas Government Code § 2001.054(c)(1) and 16 TAC §§ 22.54(a) and 22.55.

4.

Under PURA § 39.356(a) and 16 TAC § 25.1070), the Commission is authorized to
suspend, revoke, or amend a REP certificate for significant violations of PURA.

Commission rules, or ERCOT Nodal Protocols.

5.

Under PURA §39.151(i),Griddyis required to observe all scheduling. operating, planning,

reliability, and settlement policies, rules, guidelines. and procedures established by the
independent system operator in ERCOT.

6.

Under 16 TAC § 25.1070)(5), Griddy is required to maintain continuous and reliable
electric service to customers.

7.

Under 16 TAC § 25.1070)(7), Griddy is required to satisfy its financial obligations on a
reasonable and timely basis.

8.

Under 16 TAC § 25.1070)(8), Griddy is required to timely remit payment for invoiced
charges to ERCOT.

9.

Under 16 TAC § 25.107(j)(9), Griddy is required to observe any applicable scheduling,

operating, planning, reliability, and settlement policies, protocols, guidelines, procedures,
and other rules established by ERCOT.

10.

Under 16 TAC § 25.107(j)(16), Griddy is required to timely remit payments for invoiced

charges to transmission and distribution utilities in accordance with the terms of their

respective Commission-approved tariffs.
11.

Under ERCOT Nodal Protocols § 9.7.1, Griddy is required to satisfy each settlement
invoice issued by ERCOT within two bank business days of the date the settlement invoice
is issued.

12.

Under ERCOT Nodal Protocols § 16.11.5(6), Griddy is required to satisfy each collateral
call issued by ERCOT within two bank business days ofthe date the collateral call is issued.

13.

Griddy violated PURA §39.151(j) by not observing ERCOT's policies, rules, guidelines,

and procedures related to collateral calls and the payment of settlement invoices.
14.

Griddy committed a significant violation of PURA, Commission rules, or ERCOT Nodal
Protocols under 16 TAC § 25.107(j)(5) when it failed to maintain continuous and reliable
electric service to its customers.

15.

Griddy committed a significant violation of PURA, Commission rules, or ERCOT Nodal
Protocols under 16 TAC § 25.107(j)(7) by failing to satisfy its financial obligations on a

reasonable and timely basis.
16.

Griddy committed a significant violation of PURA, Commission rules, or ERCOT Nodal
Protocols under 16 TAC § 25.107(j)(8) by failing to timely remit payment for invoiced
charges to ERGOT.

17.

Griddy committed a significant violation of PURA, Commission rules, or ERCOT Nodal
Protocols under 16 TAC § 25.1070)(9) by failing to observe policies, protocols, guidelines,

procedures, and other rules established by ERCOT.
