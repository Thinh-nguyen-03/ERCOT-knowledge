ORDER GRANTING ADJUSTMENT REQUEST

On May 18. 2026. Electric Reliability Council of Texas. Inc. (ERCOT) filed with the

Public Utility Commission of Texas (Commission) a request for adjustment for the 2026
Preliminary Long-Term Load Forecast (LTLF) pursuant to 16 Texas Administrative Code (TAC)

§ 25.370(e)(2)(B). ERCOT developed the I,TI,F based on the 2026 RTP Compliance Plan. as

required by § 25.370(g). The LTLI· was developed using criteria from the 2026 RTP Compliance
Plan. which established interim criteria for the inclusion of the load of a Large Load customer in
ERCOT's load forecasts until the permanent criteria recently adopted in 16 TAC § 25.370 can be

implemented.
The I.TLF forecasts peak load is 278.003 megawatts (MW) in 2029 and 367,790 MW in
2032. In its April 15th filing in Project No. 58777.' liRCOT indicated that it had concerns with
using these values in the Reliability Assessment and any other transmission and resource adequacy

analysis. At the Commission's April 17. 2026. Open Meeting. the Commission indicated that

adjusting the I.TLF to a more realistic value may be warranted. At the May 7.2026. Open Meeting.
ERCOT confirmed to the Commission that it would propose an adjustment to the [.1[.F.
In its filing, ERCOT proposed two options for adjusting the load forecast:

Option A Methodology.

Adjustment using historical realization rates: and

Option B Methodology.

Adjustment using Batch Zero Base Load per Planning
Guide Revision Request (PGRR) 145.2

ERCOT stated in its filing that although the Option B Methodology cannot be implemented as
quickly as the Option A Methodology, Adjustment using historical realization rates, the Option B

Methodology. Adjustment using Batch Zero Base Load per PGRR 145. is expected to result in a

more reliable load forecast. ERCO-1 recommended that the benefit ofa more reliable load forecast
outweighs the cost of the additional time required.

On May 18.2026. ERCOT issued a market notice to all market participants describing the

The Option B Methodology relies on objective credible independent information. as

required by § 25.370(e)(2)(b). by using load information developed through the ERCOT

stakeholder process in PORR 145 and has been subject to significant stakeholder consultation and
input. In accordance with 16 TAC § 25.370(e)(2)(B) the Commission approves ERCOTs request
