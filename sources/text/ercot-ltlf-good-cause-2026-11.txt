ERCOT'S MOTION FOR GOOD CAUSE EXCEPTION
REGARDING 2027 MAGNITUDE
Electric Reliability Council of Texas, Inc. (ERCOT) files this Motion for a Good Cause
Exception requesting a variance from the December 1, 2026 deadline established in 16 Texas

Administrative Code (TAC) § 25.508(b)(3) in order to file the reliability standard's magnitude
update by the second quarter of 2027. A Proposed Order that would grant this request is included
as Attachment A.
I.

BACKGROUND ON RELIABILITY STANDARD MAGNITUDE

The ERCOT Region' s systemwide reliability standard, established by the Public Utility

Commission of Texas (Commission) in 16 TAC § 25.508, consists ofthree criteria: the frequency,

duration, and magnitude at which a loss of load event may occur as reflected in probabilistically
modeled simulations of the ERCOT System. The values for the frequency and duration criteria
are established in the rule while the magnitude criterion is updated on an annual basis. For the

magnitude criterion, § 25.508(b)(3) requires:
(3)

Magnitude. The expected highest level of load shed during a loss of load
event for the ERCOT region, measured as the average lost load for a given

hour, must be less than the maximum number of megawatts of load shed
that can be safely rotated during a loss of load event, as determined by
ERCOT, in consultation with commission staff and the transmission
operators, with a 1.00 percent exceedance tolerance. Beginning in 2024, on
or before December 1 of each year, ERCOT must file the maximum number

of megawatts of load shed that can be safely rotated during a loss of load
event and a summary ofthe methodology used to calculate this value.
To determine the magnitude criterion' s value, ERCOT has in prior years issued a Request
for Information (RFI) to Transmission Operators (TOs) to determine the total megawatts (MW) in

each TO's respective load shed rotation program and, of that total, how many MW can be shed at
any one time to facilitate rotation of load shed. The aggregation of TO responses to the latter RFI

Regulatory Act (PURA) §§ 37.0561(e) and 39.169.2
II.

MOTION FOR GOOD CAUSE EXCEPTION

The Commission' s rule that addresses the determination of the magnitude criterion' s value,
16 TAC § 25.508(b)(3), requires ERCOT to file by December 1 each year the maximum MW of

load shed that can be safely rotated during a loss ofload event and a summary ofthe methodology
used to calculate this value. Due to the ongoing work to finalize the Batch Zero load values,

ERCOT does not yet have a final load forecast, including an official peak load forecast for winter
2026-2027. Accordingly, ERCOT is not yet able to provide the systemwide and TO-specific total
load that TOs require to respond to RFIs to provide an updated magnitude value for 2027. For this

reason, there is good cause to grant an exception to the filing deadline in 16 TAC § 25.508(b)(3).
ERCOT anticipates that the updated magnitude could be filed no later than the second
quarter of 2027 after issuing RFIs near the end of the first quarter of 2027. Pursuant to the

Commission's authority in 16 TAC § 25.3(b) to grant an exception, ERCOT respectfully requests
a good cause exception to the December 1,2026 deadline in 16 TAC § 25.508(b)(3) to instead file

the magnitude value for 2027 by the second quarter of 2027.
ERCOT conferred with Commission Staff regarding its intention to seek this good cause

exception and is authorized to represent that Commission Staff does not oppose this request.

PROPOSED ORDER GRANTING GOOD CAUSE EXCEPTION
On November 6,2026, Electric Reliability Council of Texas, Inc. (ERCOT) filed with the

Public Utility Commission of Texas (Commission) a motion for a good cause exception pursuant
to 16 Texas Administrative Code (TAC) § 25.3(b) requesting an extension of the deadline
established by 16 TAC § 25.508(b)(3), which requires ERCOT to file by December 1, 2026 the

maximum number of megawatts ofload shed that can be safely rotated during a loss of load event

and a summary ofthe methodology used to calculate this value.
Under the authority established in 16 TAC § 25.3(b), the Commission grants ERCOT a
good cause exception to the filing deadline established by 16 TAC § 25.508(b)(3) in order for
ERCOT to instead file the updated magnitude value no later than the second quarter of 2027. Good
cause exists because the load or load forecast to be used by Transmission Operators (TOs) to

provide information about their load shed rotation capabilities for magnitude determination
purposes is not yet available. After that load or load forecast becomes available, ERCOT must use

that information to consult with the TOs and Commission Staff and file with the Commission as

soon as practicable thereafter the maximum number of megawatts of load shed that can be safely
rotated during a loss ofload event and a summary ofthe methodology used to calculate this value.
