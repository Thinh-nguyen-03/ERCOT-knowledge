What is the PUCT?
The Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) is the state agency responsible for economic
regulation of Texas' electric, telecommunication, and water and wastewater utilities.
The PUCT oversees the state's utility providers, implementing legislation and enforcing market
rules that promote reliability and high-quality infrastructure, including oversight of the Electric
Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT), which runs the electric grid for 90% of Texas' power needs.
Through rate regulation and consumer assistance, the PUCT ensures consumers across the
state are treated fairly and receive the benefits of competitive markets. The PUCT was directed
by the 88th Texas Legislature through Senate Bill 2627, the Powering Texas Forward Act, and by
the Texans who voted in the November 7, 2023, constitutional amendment election to
administer the TxEF.

What is the Texas Energy Fund (TxEF)?
The TxEF provides grants and loans to finance the construction, maintenance, modernization,
and operation of electric facilities in Texas. The Texas Legislature passed Senate Bill 2627, the
Powering Texas Forward Act, to create the TxEF, which Texans approved through a
constitutional election on November 7, 2023. The 89th Texas Legislature then passed Senate Bill
2268 to adjust certain financing requirements, programmatic caps, and deadlines for loans and
grants awarded from the TxEF.

What are the different TxEF programs?
The TxEF is made up of four programs that provide loan and grant opportunities for electric
generation projects inside and outside the ERCOT power region: 1) the In-ERCOT Generation
Loan Program; 2) the Completion Bonus Grant Program; 3) the Outside ERCOT Grant Program;
and 4) the Texas Backup Power Package Program. Each program is guided by a set of rules
drafted with public input and adopted by the Commission.
In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program
The In-ERCOT Generation Loan (Loan) Program provides three percent-interest loans with a
term of 20 years to qualifying companies for the construction of new or expansion of existing
dispatchable electric generating facilities in the ERCOT power region. Qualifying projects must
add at least 100 MW of new dispatchable generation capacity, which is output that can be
controlled primarily by forces under human control. Projects involving resources such as solar,
wind, and hydroelectric are considered non-dispatchable and therefore ineligible for the
program.

On March 21, 2024, the PUCT adopted 16 TAC §25.510, which establishes the Loan Program
application process, project eligibility requirements, evaluation criteria, and loan terms. The
PUCT began accepting Loan Program applications on June 1, 2024, and closed the submission
period on July 27, 2024. High-level information on Loan Program activity to date is listed on the
TxEF website, and detailed information and resources for Loan Program applicants and
borrowers is available on the TxEF Online website.
Completion Bonus Grant Program
New generation resources at a new or existing electric generating facility may be eligible for a
Completion Bonus Grant (CBG) if they interconnect to the ERCOT power grid before June 1,
2029. Eligible projects must provide at least 100 megawatts (MW) of new, dispatchable
nameplate capacity in the ERCOT region, among other criteria. Dispatchable capacity is output
that can be controlled primarily by forces under human control.
On April 25, 2024, the PUCT adopted 16 TAC §25.511, which establishes the CBG Program
application process, project eligibility requirements, and evaluation criteria. The PUCT began
accepting CBG Program applications on January 1, 2025, and continues to accept new
applications. High-level information on CBG Program activity to date is listed on the TxEF
website, and detailed information and resources for CBG Program applicants and grantees is
available on the TxEF Online website.
Outside ERCOT Grant Program
The Outside ERCOT Grant Program (OEGP) provides funding to existing transmission and
distribution infrastructure and existing electric generating facilities in Texas outside of the
ERCOT power region. Projects under the OEGP must meet one of the program objectives:
facility modernization, facility weatherization, reliability and resiliency, and vegetation
management. Construction of a new electric generation resource is considered an ineligible
activity under this program, among other exclusions.
On February 13, 2025, the PUCT adopted 16 TAC §25.512, which establishes the OEGP
application process, project eligibility requirements, and evaluation criteria. The PUCT began
accepting OEGP applications on May 28, 2025, and continues to accept new applications. Highlevel information on OEGP activity to date is listed on the TxEF website, and detailed
information and resources for OEGP applicants and grantees is available on the TxEF Online
website.
Texas Backup Power Package Program
The Texas Backup Power Package (TBPP) Program will provide grants and loans to qualifying
entities to design, procure, install, and operate backup power packages at critical facilities that
support community health, safety, and well-being. Examples of critical facilities include but are
not limited to hospitals, fire stations, assisted living facilities, cooling centers, and gas stations

source that connects directly to a critical facility without passing through a utility meter.
On May 7, 2026, the PUCT adopted 16 TAC §25.513, which establishes the TBPP Program
application process, project eligibility requirements, and evaluation criteria. The PUCT will begin
accepting TBPP applications in 2027. The PUCT has facilitated multiple opportunities for the
public to provide input on TBPP research reports, program design, and vendor procurement.
High-level information on TBPP Program activity to date is listed on the TxEF website, and
detailed information and resources for TBPP Program applicants will be available on the TxEF
Online website.

Who is eligible to apply to each program?
Eligibility requirements are different per program.
For In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program eligibility, refer to 16 TAC §25.510(c).
For Completion Bonus Grant Program eligibility, refer to 16 TAC §25.511(c).
For Outside ERCOT Grant Program eligibility, refer to 16 TAC §25.512(b).
For Texas Backup Power Package Program eligibility, refer to 16 TAC §25.513(b).

What are the key dates and deadlines for the TxEF programs?
Key dates for each TxEF program are summarized on the TxEF website. Visit TxEF Online for
detailed resources for applicants and awardees, organized by program.

How do I learn more and apply to TxEF programs?
For general information about each TxEF program, visit the PUCT’s TxEF website to view
program descriptions, awardee information and links to rules, Interchange filings and press
releases.
For detailed resources on preparing to apply for a TxEF program, visit TxEF Online to view
program-specific Frequently Asked Questions, program manuals, video tutorials, and user
guides for forms and reports.
To upload documents and submit an application for a TxEF program, log into the TxEF Portal.

Who do I contact for more information?
For more information or assistance, contact the Texas Energy Fund Administrator by phone at
1-866-526-2269 or email at info@txenergyfund.texas.gov.
