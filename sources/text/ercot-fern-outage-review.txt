Part II: Inspection Process Refresher

Under 16 TAC §25.55(d)(1)(A) and (g)(1)(A):
Generation entities and TSPs must submit all requirements for accessing their facilities to ERCOT,
commission staff, and designated contractors no later than 24 hours after receiving an inspection notice.

ServiceNow elapsed time is measured in hours.

Email or a support request may document a problem, but it does not replace timely portal acceptance.

Key takeaway: Make sure someone can complete the unique-link portal acknowledgement within 24 hours; emails and
support requests do not replace the required portal action.

During the inspection: arrival, review, and walkthrough
Inspector compares documentation to field conditions and confirms weatherization readiness.

Inspection step

What to expect

Arrival and site entry

Inspector checks in with the designated site contact and confirms safety requirements.

Initial meeting

Introductions, site conditions, access requirements, and inspection scope are confirmed.

Documentation review

Inspector reviews documents provided in the portal and available on-site. This can be
expedited if documentation was provided in the portal.

Site walkthrough

Inspector examines weather-critical components, support systems, insulation,
enclosures, and heating/cooling systems.

Wrap-up and follow-up

Inspector summarizes key findings and immediate concerns before leaving the site.

Keep knowledgeable personnel available and be prepared to explain procedures, training, staffing coverage, maintenance cycles,
and extreme-weather response.

Key takeaway: During the inspection, be ready to connect records, site conditions, and staff knowledge in real time.
