6 LOAD PROFILING METHODOLOGY 6-1

 LOAD PROFILING METHODOLOGY

(1) Any changes to the Load Profiling Methodology shall be submitted as a Load Profiling Guide Revision Request (LPGRR) as described in Section 2.4, Load Profiling Guide Revision Procedure. There shall be no retroactive application of any approved modifications to Load Profiling Methodology.
